Episode 13: Master of the Jade Staff

Heading south, Kadan(041.png) and Celia(062.png) smelled grilling meat on the forest road. The owner of the scent was Leonar(086.png), an old warrior with a huge lion's mane.

He lay down with his green jade staff as a pillow and freely evaluated the meal the two had prepared. Instead of getting angry, Celia(062.png) looked at the remaining ingredients. There were only dried mushrooms, tough boar meat, and sour berries, but the wind divided the temperature of the fire and let the herbal scent seep into the meat.

Leonar(086.png) took his first bite and opened his eyes wide. Leonar(086.png) did not say that it was delicious, but criticized the cooking of the meat and the amount of salt.

When Celia(062.png) tried to snatch the plate away, he swallowed the last piece whole. Kadan(041.png) laughed when he saw that, but even his share was taken away by Leonar(086.png). The teacher-disciple relationship between the three began across the dining table.

“What is the price? ” “Teach my friend an attack technique other than a shield. ” Kadan(041.png) was surprised that Celia(062.png) asked for something before him.

Leonar(086.png) looked at Kadan(041.png)'s shoulders and feet and commented that he had enough strength but did not know where to stop his strength. What he taught was the first of the eighteen Dragon Breaker seals.

It was not a technique that caused a large explosion of mana. It was a method of accurately delivering a single impact while leaving the force to move back. Kadan(041.png) couldn't shake a single tree for three days.

The leaves fell off on the 10th day, and after a month, only the rotted parts inside the trunk were pushed out without breaking the tree. Leonar(086.png) admitted that he had finally learned his first seal.

Celia(062.png) did not just watch the water lilies. The point at which Kadan(041.png)'s mana leaks is indicated by the temperature of the wind. At first, there were too many instructions, which made Kadan(041.png)'s movements more complicated, but the two reduced the signals to three.

Left, pause, breath. It was a language that would later be used faster than hundreds of words on the battlefield. A swarm of Medusa Gorgo(089.png) snake spirits flocked to that forest.

The spirits swelled by sucking the mana of living beasts, and the white-cloaked trainers used flutes to change direction. Leonar(086.png) drew a line on the ground with a green jade staff, creating a barrier that snakes could not cross.

Celia(062.png) was bitten by two small snakes, but the windscale armor he wore under his clothes repelled his teeth. Kadan(041.png) tried to freeze the snake swarm with his frost mana, which was immune to poison. Leonar(086.png) stopped him.

[Leonar(086.png), Celia(062.png), Kadan(041.png)] “If you kill everyone because they are easy to kill, you will fight in a way determined by the enemy. ” The three found the frequency of the training flute and turned it over with the wind.

The snake spirits turned towards their owners and the trainers ran away. Although Medusa Gorgo(089.png) did not appear, a revenge emblem directed at Aeolus(025.png) was scratched on the tree bark. Leonar(086.png) was a wandering leader of the Freemen's League.

An organization where wanderers and refugees, not kings or nobles, feed each other. The green jade staff was the key to proving the leader. He looked at Celia(062.png)'s judgment and Kadan(041.png)'s restraint.

On the day of leaving, Leonar(086.png) promised to teach Kadan(041.png) the remaining seventeen seals in the future. The conditions were not grand.

Even after gaining strength, sit at the same table as hungry people. Leonar(086.png) never revealed the name of the first seal. The reason was that if you learn the name first, people will imitate the legend rather than the movement.

Kadan(041.png) called it the hand that leaves behind the strength to return the way it was created. Leonar(086.png) laughed and said it was tacky, but did not change it.

He left heavier words for Celia(062.png). “Daughter of the Wind Island, the day will never come when you become a leader because of your bloodline. But if people trust your judgment and hand you a staff, do not run away.

” Kadan(041.png) did not ask why Celia(062.png) suddenly became silent. Instead, Pegasus(090.png) slowed down and walked side by side with her. At the end of the forest, a huge lake of fog spread out in silver light under the sunlight.
