Episode 34: The Fallen Wind Isle

[Kadan(041.png)] Whisper of the Wind Island no longer hides behind the fog. All the barrier towers had collapsed and the forest was burned black.

The trail, which was always changing, became straight like a dead snake. As soon as Celia(062.png) landed on the beach, he ran, calling people's names. Instead of answering, only the broken wind chime bell rang.

Most of the island's residents retreated to underground shelters, but traces of those who guarded the barrier remained everywhere. In the central garden, Kadan(041.png) found Grimli(074.png)'s hammer. The silver bell of Robinyard(048.png) was still attached to the handle.

Going a little further, Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png)'s crossbow, Kaze(044.png)'s sword, and Rondol(046.png)'s oar were laid out one after another. The weapons weren't dropped randomly.

Grimli(074.png)'s hammer was supporting the entrance to the shelter, and Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png)'s last arrow pointed in the opposite direction from where the children fled. Kaze(044.png)'s knife was stuck with the alarm cord cut. Teachers created someone's path until the moment they died.

The six masters arrived on the island first, chasing Death Knight Artus(060.png)'s army. Only Gawain(045.png) survived with fatal injuries. He heard Kadan(041.png)'s footsteps with his invisible eyes and stretched out his hand.

[Death Knight Artus(060.png), Gawain(045.png), Kadan(041.png)] “It’s not too late. We just got here first.

” Gawain(045.png) said the attacker used Aeolus(025.png)'s flute sound. In the black fog, it was impossible to tell who was real, and the comrades fell one by one while protecting each other. At the last moment, I even saw the figure of Aeolus(025.png) directly wielding the soul sword.

He said that Grimli(074.png) raised his shield at the end, imitating Kadan(041.png)'s posture. He was a person who always found his student's defense frustrating. Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png) used his crossbow like a club after running out of arrows, and even at the last moment, Kaze(044.png) left funny graffiti on the enemy's armor.

Kadan(041.png) knelt before the weapons of his teachers. No tears came out. All I could think about was what kind of curse Grimli(074.png) would have sworn at the end and how many shots Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png) had left.

Aeolus(025.png) emerged from the ruins of the temple. There was blood on the clothes and the flute.

He said everyone was already passed out when he arrived. Kadan(041.png) wanted to believe it, but Gawain(045.png)'s testimony and traces of the flute were right in front of him. Celia(062.png) approached his father and asked for the flute.

The moment Aeolus(025.png) hesitated for a moment, Kadan(041.png) accepted the movement as evidence of guilt. The impact of the Dragon Breaker split the garden floor. Aeolus(025.png) did not counterattack, but instead attacked with a flute.

[Aeolus(025.png), Kadan(041.png)] “There is no room for any answers to questions asked in anger. ” “Then why do you avoid it?

” “Because if you kill me now, Celia(062.png) will collapse twice. ” Those words made Kadan(041.png) even more angry. When the battle between the two began, Celia(062.png) could not get in between them.

Kadan(041.png)'s sadness at seeing the bodies of his teachers and his desire to believe that his father was not the culprit were all real. Gawain(045.png) rang a silver bell with his last breath. The sound was slightly out of sync with Aeolus(025.png)'s flute.

This meant that the flute heard during the attack was not a perfect replica, but was half a beat late. Before he could say that, Gawain(045.png)'s hand fell.

Kadan(041.png) held the silver bell, but did not immediately understand its meaning. Aeolus(025.png) retreated into the black fog, saying that if he fought any longer, the truth would disappear forever. Celia(062.png) did not follow Kadan(041.png).

The first priority was to hold the funerals of teachers and rescue refugees. For the first time, the two people chose different paths in the face of the same grief. Celia(062.png) did not light the funeral fire all at once.

The refugees were asked to tell each teacher what they remembered. Some people talked about the pot that Grimli(074.png) fixed, and some people talked about the knot that Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png) taught them. It was a funeral that did not reduce the death of a hero to a single battle scene.
