Episode 23: The Record Sleeping in the Cave

Elwin(042.png) The village was frozen as it was the day it was burned. Underneath the collapsed tavern, there was an underground passage that neither Aleksandar(050.png) nor Balian(043.png) knew about.

Lucian(061.png) and Death Knight Artus(060.png) were digging through the ruins to find the strategic core left behind by the kingdom's war hero, Ironforge(080.png). The core of Ironforge(080.png) recorded not only the tactics of protecting a single castle wall, but also the continent's ley lines, supply routes, and a refuge network for moving civilians. If Lucian(061.png) got his hands on it, he could destroy Wildland and Lighthaven at the same time.

Residents of the ruins believed the treasure was gold or weapons. Kadan(041.png) wondered if telling the truth would put people in more danger, but Celia(062.png) said the residents needed to know what was beneath their village.

Before entering the cave, they held a village meeting to explain the risks and options. Instead of running away, residents took over guarding the exit. Kadan(041.png) and the party entered the cave with Orihalcon(038.png) as their guide.

A long time ago, while working as a royal engineer, he created a magnetic device for the entrance. Celia(062.png) knew he would cheat again, so he put wind marks on both sleeves. When the heart beats faster while lying, the sign makes a whistle.

[Celia(062.png)] The inside of the cave was not a tomb, but a huge mock battlefield. Thousands of stone soldiers moved according to the invaders' choices.

Every time Kadan(041.png) chose to attack head-on, the refugee model fell first. In the first mock battle, Kadan(041.png) was declared defeated even after defeating the enemy commander. This is because one stone child was left behind in the evacuation procession.

The second time, we lost our supply route while waiting for everyone. Celia(062.png) found small letters on the edge of the device, saying he had to read the victory conditions themselves.

“This is not a test to find a strong commander. ” Celia(062.png) said. “It’s a test to find a way to get someone out without a fight.

” She opened the old aqueduct and sent an alarm to the other side with the wind. Kadan(041.png) drew back the stone soldier's attack with his shield, and Leonar(086.png) marked the retreat route with his green jade staff. When the three people divided up their roles, the door to the mock battlefield opened for the first time.

[Leonar(086.png), Kadan(041.png)] There was a black stone box in the center. The lid was engraved with the phrase, “Count those who survive before you win.

” When Kadan(041.png) touched it, his father's dagger and stone chest resonated. Aleksandar(050.png) was one of the people who hid Ironforge(080.png)'s last messenger. The strategic core inside the stone box was not a crystal sphere but hundreds of thin metal leaves.

Each chapter contained memories from a different region, so removing one changed the entire strategy. Celia(062.png) argued that it should be distributed and stored like a codex.

Death Knight Artus(060.png) came through the wall. A black wind swirled through his empty armor. Kadan(041.png)'s attack passed through without finding the body, and Leonar(086.png)'s barrier was cut off by the soul sword.

Orihalcon(038.png) was able to escape, but activated the stone box's magnetic device. Death Knight Artus(060.png)'s armor stuck to the wall and its movement stopped. He laughed and said he had become a hero, but he knew better than anyone else that the device would not last long.

When Kadan(041.png) tried to stay together, Orihalcon(038.png) was the first to swear harshly. “If you want to brag about saving me for the rest of your life, don’t die here with me.

” Celia(062.png) grabbed Kadan(041.png) by the collar and dragged him into the aisle. It was a face that did not want to make anyone's sacrifice a romantic ending. Celia(062.png) divided the metal leaves into three bundles.

Leonar(086.png) was in charge of the refuge net, Kadan(041.png) was in charge of defensive tactics, and he was in charge of the ley line map. The last one was handed to Orihalcon(038.png).

“You protect it as proof that this choice was not a trick. ” Those words silenced Orihalcon(038.png)'s bravado. He placed the metal leaf inside the armor and held onto Death Knight Artus(060.png)'s magnetic device until the end.

The moment everyone exited the passage, the device exploded and half the cave collapsed. Death Knight Artus(060.png) rose from the rubble but was unable to obtain the entire strategic core. Outside the ruins, Kadan(041.png) realized that what his father was trying to protect was not a family or revenge, but an escape route for ordinary people.
