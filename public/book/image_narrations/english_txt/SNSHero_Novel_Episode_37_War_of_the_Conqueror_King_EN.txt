Episode 37: War of the Conqueror King

Across the lower bastions and jagged ravines of the Black Citadel, a savage contest raged between King Fenrir's Silver Wolf cavalry and Chancellor Lucian's final elite guard, the Spectral Knights. Massive silver wolves forged in the permafrost of the northern wastes lunged across the sulfur bogs, tearing into the throats of undead riders, while King Fenrir's greatsword cleaved through black-iron plate with incandescent sparks.

King Fenrir rested his bloodstained broadsword upon his broad shoulder, his thunderous voice reverberating across the battlefield.

"Do the sons of the north fear the spilling of blood? Brother Aleksandar, watch from the heavens! The wolves of the tundra never forsake their oaths! Do not allow a single fiend to ascend toward Kadan until the throne is shattered!"

Northern Vanguard Captain Torvald gripped the coarse mane of his mount, raising his lance in answer.

"Brothers, show them the fangs of the wolf pack! Grant no quarter until the young paladin brings down the tyrant!"

Thousands of northern warriors banged their axes against their shields, their battle cries shaking the citadel walls and sealing the upper galleries against enemy reinforcements.

Hearing the distant roar of battle at their backs, Kadan and Princess Cilia pushed forward through the upper arched colonnade. As they neared the inner threshold, Lucian's dread champion, Blood Knight Karnak, stepped into the corridor at the head of a hundred crimson-armored shock troopers. Karnak's flesh had been warped by injections of Medusa Gorgo's necrotic blood, transforming him into a hulking brute whose hands swung a colossal broadsword carved from dragon bone.

Karnak bared his fangs, his bloodshot eyes burning with unholy madness.

"This corridor is the beating heart of the citadel! Traitors, you shall not advance a single pace! Your flesh shall feed the sacrificial altar!"

The hundred blood-armored soldiers lowered their spears, filling the narrow corridor like an iron tide. Cilia swept her jade staff, launching a crescent of compressed wind, but the strike shattered against the noxious crimson barrier conjured by Karnak's bone blade.

"Ordinary wind cannot pierce their lines! Their blood is bound by Gorgo's vile sorcery!"

Kadan stepped forward, lowering the Dragon Breaker shield into a resolute defensive wedge.

"Bonds forged in bloodshed crumble before the conviction to preserve life."

Kadan regulated his core through star-breathing, anchoring his stance to the bedrock. When Karnak's massive bone sword slammed down upon the adamantite face of the shield, Kadan did not merely absorb the blow; he twisted his guard, unleashing the full kinetic recoil of the earth.

A blinding golden dragon materialized from the shield's starlight core, sending a devastating shockwave tearing through the corridor. The impact shattered Karnak's bone broadsword into splinters, hurling his massive frame through the air, while the concussive burst swept the hundred shock troopers across the flagstones, disarming them in a single stroke.

Cilia unleashed a swirling gale that swept the discarded weapons over the parapet into the chasm below, neutralizing the elite vanguard without shedding innocent blood.

Kadan drew a calm breath, adjusting his shield.

"Our crusade seeks not slaughter, but the salvation of mortal lives."

Kneeling in the rubble, Karnak coughed black bile, staring at Kadan's clear, unblemished gaze with tears of bitter repentance. Seeing the serene purity of the paladin's shield, he recognized how wretched his lust for brute power had been.

"Your shield is truly... an unyielding mountain to sustain this world... Behind Lucian's throne... lie the Three Silk Seals of the Nine Shadows... I beg of you, destroy that darkness..."

Kadan placed a firm hand upon the fallen warrior's shoulder.

"I have heard your repentance. Lay down your arms and await the breaking of the dawn."

Karnak bowed his head in reverent humility. At that moment, a northern messenger falcon descended from a fissure in the vaulted ceiling. Tied to its talon was a brief missive in Fenrir's bold hand: 'The beast host below is annihilated. Lucian's retreat is severed. Strike down the throne without hesitation!'

Releasing the falcon, Kadan turned to Cilia. Both wore expressions of unyielding resolve. Kadan clasped the warm glass vessel holding Ignis's ember within his coat, drawing strength from its steady pulse.

Cilia leveled her staff toward the inner hall.

"King Fenrir and our northern kin hold the path. Let us face Lucian's throne."

Kadan took the lead, stepping into the crimson glow of the throne room.

"Come, Cilia. Let us end this eighteen-year night and greet the morning sun."
