Episode 33: Grudge of the Black Marsh

Naiad(002.png) did not give up on revenge. However, the target has changed.

Instead of killing Aqualord(005.png), he went deep into the black swamp to find Death Knight Artus(060.png), who had taken Aquaris(001.png)'s life force. Gandar(069.png) followed her without saying a word. Kadan(041.png) and Celia(062.png) also went together.

Death Knight Artus(060.png) was injecting artificial souls into armor in a lung laboratory beneath the swamp. On the wall, the mana waveforms of Aquaris(001.png), Ignis(011.png), and numerous nameless test subjects were hung like samples.

Celia(062.png) gave each specimen a temporary name. The name was created based on the rooms and the order in which they were discovered, but the intention was not to leave them as numbers. Kadan(041.png) took off the metal plates one by one and put them into his backpack.

Even after the enemy was defeated, it remained to reveal who had been sacrificed. When Naiad(002.png) saw Aquaris(001.png)'s waveform, he forgot all calculations and rushed forward.

Death Knight Artus(060.png) waited for that anger. The soul sword used strong emotions as a conduit to invade the opponent's memories. Naiad(002.png) ended up seeing the night Aquaris(001.png) died over and over again.

Gandar(069.png) tried to pull her away, but he was also trapped in the same memory. The possibility that things would have been different if he had not left, the life that Aquaris(001.png) might have called his father unfolded as an illusion.

Celia(062.png) used the green jade staff to sever the connection between the two people's emotions and Death Knight Artus(060.png). Instead of attacking the armor, Kadan(041.png) destroyed the soul storage tube on the lab floor. Nameless lights came out all at once and went up to the swamp.

Death Knight Artus(060.png)'s armor began to empty. He has maintained his body through hundreds of souls.

As Kadan(041.png) released them one by one, the movement became slower. In the last storage tube, there was a resonance left by Aquaris(001.png). She tried to put it into her body.

If Aqualord(005.png) said it, Aquaris(001.png)'s voice could be heard even for a moment. The reservoir was ready to produce the voice that Naiad(002.png) most wanted to hear. It was not a real memory, but a device that simulated sadness.

Even if Celia(062.png) found out about it, he did not immediately destroy it. In order not to lose the choice, I explained the structure to Naiad(002.png) and waited.

Gandar(069.png) held his hand. “Don’t lock Aquaris(001.png) inside you again just because we want to see you. ” Naiad(002.png) held the storage tube in his arms for a long time and then opened the lid.

[Aquaris(001.png), Gandar(069.png), Naiad(002.png)] The water-colored resonance rose into the sky and circled around the two. There were no words or faces, but a warm presence touched the back of my hand for a moment.

Death Knight Artus(060.png) escaped and flooded the laboratory. Celia(062.png) created wind bubbles to protect everyone's breath, and Kadan(041.png) escaped carrying the last storage tubes on his shoulder. I could have finished it by pursuing the enemy, but I saved the trapped soul first.

Outside the swamp, Naiad(002.png) wrote a letter to Aqualord(005.png). There was no word of forgiveness.

It was said that he had poisoned himself and that he would stay alive and see what he did. At the end of the letter, the name Aquaris(001.png) was written for the first time. During my revenge, I hid my name out of fear that I would become weak if I spoke of it.

Gandar(069.png) folded the letter without reading the name out loud. I decided to wait until Naiad(002.png) spoke.

Gandar(069.png) did not declare that he would remain by her side. I just asked if I wanted to walk together the next day. Instead of answering, Naiad(002.png) put half of his luggage on his back.

The records obtained from the research center indicated that the next attack target was Whisper of the Wind Island. Death Knight Artus(060.png) attempted to take over the entire continent's communication network using Aeolus(025.png)'s sound runes. Kadan(041.png) and Celia(062.png) headed toward the eastern sea without any time to recover.
