Episode 09: Wind in Beggar's Clothes

Upon the timber platform of Lighthaven's inner forum, tension solidified into razor-sharp ice. Crimson silk standards fluttered from the perimeter parapets, and from gilded balconies overhead, noblewomen with ivory fans and courtiers with perfumed beards looked down in avid curiosity. The throng of common folk held their breath, their gazes darting between Kadan—a nameless youth robed in rugged northern furs bearing an unadorned iron shield—and Ignis, the celebrated young champion of the realm gleaming in immaculate white-silver plate. Sulfur smoke from the previous bout seeped through the flagstone fissures, and charred remnants of protective brambles drifted on the chilly autumn gale like gray snow.

The court herald blew his gilded trumpet, his voice booming over the assembly.

"A vagabond wretch from the Wildlands dares challenge the sacred honor of the White Flame Order! Witness the immaculate judgment of High Commander Ignis!"

Ignis's golden eyes gleamed with haughty condescension as he inspected Kadan's battered shield. A cold sneer curved his lips.

"A nameless vagabond dares trespass upon an imperial duel? You court your own execution. That rusted iron lid shall serve as your headstone!"

Ignis raised his longsword in a high guard, and pure white sanctifying flames roared to life along the steel like a cataract of liquid fire. Descending with thunderous force, the opening gambit, 'Incandescent Flash,' struck like lightning. A blinding sheet of white brilliance cleaved the air, yet Kadan did not yield half a pace. Drawing upon the foundation taught by Master Aleksandar, he tilted the rim of his iron shield at an obtuse angle, deflecting the kinetic axis of the strike. With a deafening clang, arcs of white flame scattered harmlessly into the empty air.

Shocked that his opening stroke was turned aside, Ignis's expression hardened into granite. Without pausing, he unleashed his second technique, 'Solar Cleave,' sending a fissure of roaring white flame across the arena stones. The granite flagstones split apart with volcanic fury, yet Kadan grounded his weight through star-breathing, redirecting the devastating shockwave into the perimeter stone pillars.

As successive attacks failed before the eyes of the gathered aristocracy, Ignis's pride gave way to dark fury. Channeling forbidden mana, he summoned the parasitic life-drain claw imparted in secret by Medusa Gorgo. Blackish-crimson runes coiled around Ignis's gauntleted fingers, slashing violently across Kadan's left shoulder. With a sickening hiss, the cured leather armor burned away, dark crimson blood welling through the scorched tear. A foul stench of necrotic decay rose from the charred flesh.

Watching from the front ranks of the crowd, Cilia snapped her fingers with lightning speed.

"Subterranean wards are synchronizing with his mana! Disengage, Kadan!"

Manipulating subterranean air currents, Cilia severed the tracking magic circles concealed beneath the flagstones. Microscopic needles of compressed wind punctured the hidden ley conduits, paralyzing the imperial mages' sensory wards in a burst of azure sparks.

At that exact moment, an imposing warrior bearing the sapphire mantle of the Arcane Holy Knights leaped onto the platform like a bolt of lightning. It was Paladin Walker.

Walker brought down his jade-encrusted staff, parrying Ignis's flaming blade with a resounding boom.

"Who taught you to employ that vile parasitic claw? You dare sully the immaculate flame of the Holy Order with the necrotic sorcery of fiends? Shame upon your steel!"

Yet Gorgo's insidious poison leaped from the blade, creeping up Walker's staff. A pale gray shadow of necrotic venom spread across Walker's temples, causing the veteran to stumble. The forum erupted into pandemonium as watch horns wailed and armored guards surged forward with drawn crossbows. Cilia raised a colossal gale wall, swatting aside the incoming hail of bolts. Grasping the wounded Paladin Walker beneath his arms, Kadan and Cilia melted into the labyrinthine alleys of the lower city.

Inside an abandoned timber windmill on the outskirts of the watermills, Paladin Walker collapsed upon a pallet of dry straw, his ragged gasps shuddering through the gloom. Blood dripping from his wound crystallized into dark frost the instant it touched the floorboards.

Cilia cast aside her soiled beggar's mantle. Beneath the rags emerged a breathtaking tunic of teal silk and a delicate circlet of braided wind-silver. Through locks of flowing silver-blue hair peered the slender, pointed ears of high elven lineage. She was none other than Princess Cilia of Whispering Wind Island, sovereign realm of the eastern seas. Even the dust motes in the abandoned mill seemed to bow before her serene aura.

Panting softly, Cilia inspected Kadan's lacerated shoulder.

"It is Gorgo's life-drain venom. If left untreated, your heart will turn to solid stone within three sunrises."

Despite the agony coursing through his veins, Kadan offered a faint, gentle smile.

"Those silk robes look far too thin for the northern winds. You must have been shivering all this time."

Cilia glared at him in disbelief, yet glistening tears welled at the corners of her eyes.

"Is my attire your primary concern right now? Foolish boy, why did you leap into another's duel only to suffer this horrific wound!"

Breathing heavily from the straw pallet, Paladin Walker spoke with strained rasp.

"Eighteen winters ago, beneath the howling blizzard of Elwin hamlet, Brother Aleksandar and Brother Balian touched the flats of their blades above your cradles. Their sacred vow to defend human dignity and justice remained unbroken before any darkness. Yet corrupt lords of the court and Death Knight Artus orchestrated their betrayal, and infant Ignis was stolen away to be raised as a gilded puppet of the Flame Court."

Kadan tightened his calloused fists. The bedtime lullabies his mother Elwin had sung on frozen winter nights finally aligned with the harsh reality before his eyes.

"Then Ignis is not my sworn enemy... but a brother with whom I must share the truth."

Walker rested a blood-stained gauntlet upon Kadan's shoulder.

"Indeed. To strike him down with steel is simple, but to liberate his soul from the fetters of shadow requires a heart bound to the true oath. To purge this venom, you require the Moonlit Herb—an ancient panacea guarded exclusively inside the royal apothecary. Yet that sanctum is sealed behind three tiers of impenetrable imperial wards, and beneath it operate horrific alchemical contraptions extracting the living mana of captive souls."

Kadan withdrew his mother's keepsake, the silver dagger.

"The runes upon this silver blade can unravel any lock. I shall breach the vault."

Cilia blocked the doorway, shaking her head.

"You cannot go alone. The subterranean exhaust shafts of the royal gardens can only be navigated through the guidance of wind sorcery."

Under cover of midnight gloom, the two crept into the imperial conservatory. The sewer waterways reeked of rotting lichen and pungent sludge. Cilia wove a continuous sound-dampening breeze that erased their footfalls, while Kadan channeled the frost mana of the silver dagger, flash-freezing the internal tumbler pins of the heavy iron vaults until they snapped cleanly. Pushing open the massive bronze portals, the radiant azure aroma of the Moonlit Herb greeted their senses, glowing softly in the dark. Growing inside frosty glass domes, the crystalline leaves shimmered with self-luminous starlight.

As they gathered the healing herbs, Kadan's gaze snagged upon an iron dispatch coffer stamped with red wax in the corner. Opening the docket, Kadan's heart ceased beating for an agonizing second.

'Seventh Subterranean Labor Camp: Former Grand Master Balian, living mana extraction trials ongoing.'

Uncle Balian still lived. Within Kadan's breast, the oath sworn beneath the stars flared into an incandescent flame.

"Uncle Balian survives... I swear upon my blood to tear down his chains."

Cilia rested a hand upon Kadan's uninjured shoulder, nodding with solemn determination.

"Let us brew the draught to heal Paladin Walker, then march upon the labor camp at once. The winds shall be our unfailing guide."

Moonlight poured through the skylights, illuminating the path of two young heroes stepping forward across the capital's dark expanse. The profound weight of the ancient vow enveloped their shoulders, and with every passing shadow, the nascent dawn they sought to usher in burned ever brighter.
