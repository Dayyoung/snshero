Episode 09: Wind in Beggars Clothes

When Kadan(041.png)'s snow crystal shield and Ignis(011.png)'s white flame collided, the duel's rune stone split in half. Ignis(011.png)'s technique was fast and flashy, but the bigger the attack, the more gaps appeared in the next move. Kadan(041.png) survived the gap by connecting different beats learned from six teachers.

As the game dragged on, Ignis(011.png) raised his fingers like hooks. It was a life force extraction rune passed down by Medusa Gorgo(089.png). Kadan(041.png) remembered the sign of the silver whip he had seen in the cave and turned his body, but his shoulder was deeply torn.

As Kadan(041.png)'s blood fell on the floor of the duel table, the hidden tracking symbol glowed red. It was a device that recorded the participants' mana so that the royal army could later find it. Celia(062.png) sent wind down the stands, breaking the lines of the pattern one by one.

While Kadan(041.png) was fighting, she first noticed that the purpose of the duel was not winning or losing. It was the paladin Walker(051.png) who stopped the duel. He set up a blue barrier between the two and grabbed Ignis(011.png)'s wrist.

Walker(051.png), a member of the same Star Sanctuary as Hercul(049.png), was astonished to see Ignis(011.png) using both the Light Sword and Forbidden Arts that he had learned from his disciple. “Who taught you to do that? ” Ignis(011.png) did not reply.

As the palace guards approached, he left the duel, saying only that he would not forget the insult. Kadan(041.png) supported Balbo(071.png) and Eliana(063.png), who had fallen. At that time, he heard six teachers calling out to him from among the crowd, but the moment he turned around, the people had already dispersed.

Walker(051.png) was poisoned by Ignis(011.png)'s attack. The moon leaves needed for treatment were only found in the palace apothecary. When Kadan(041.png) said he was going to enter the front door, Celia(062.png) sighed. “Why is the only part of your plan that involves breaking down the door?

Celia(062.png) was the daughter of Aeolus(025.png), who ruled Whisper of the Wind Island. Celia(062.png) has prepared many situations in which Kadan(041.png) will get angry. He first revealed how he hid his identity, tested his words, and even joked about asking for Pegasus(090.png).

” Celia(062.png) didn't get any of the apples he expected. The simplicity of Kadan(041.png) has sometimes most easily broken down a clever person's defenses. Kadan(041.png) forgot what to say for a while.

Celia(062.png) raised his eyebrows and barely opened his mouth. “You must have been cold in those clothes. ” Celia(062.png) laughed and wiped the corners of his eyes.

That answer was more like Kadan(041.png) than saying I was beautiful or deceived. From a young age, she received the vigilance and admiration of everyone on her father's island. He had just run away because he wanted to see how someone treated him when his identity was unknown.

The two sneaked into the pharmacy. Celia(062.png) sent wind into the vent to eavesdrop on the guard's conversation, and Kadan(041.png) stopped the vibration of the alarm rune with a thin film of ice. As I was about to turn around to look for the moon leaves, a fragrant bag slipped down the shelf. In the pharmacy, confiscated herbs were piled up like a mountain and rotting.

City doctors send patients away because they cannot find even a single antipyretic herb, but the palace keeps them in a warehouse to raise the price. Celia(062.png) changed his plan to take only the moonlight leaves. I unsealed all the boxes and threw a note with the location written on it out the window.
