SNSHero Novel
Episode 15: Night of Serpent and Lion

A skull-shaped crystal box was delivered to the Cloud Return Mansion. Five finger prints were deeply engraved on the lid, and on the inside, the emblem of Medusa Gorgo(089.png), which said to come back in three days, glowed.

Rondol(046.png)'s face turned pale. In the past, he was the one who chased down Medusa Gorgo(089.png) and Belphegor(013.png) under the orders of Aeolus(025.png). Rondol(046.png) attempted to send his family and refugees off the island.

Since it was a grudge he had created, he thought he would handle it alone. Celia(062.png) opposed the decision. He shot back that his attitude of keeping secrets to himself was the same as Aeolus(025.png).

The residents of the mansion also did not leave. Rondol(046.png) thought he was protecting them, but the fishermen, potters, and children already considered this place their home. After hearing that no one had asked him to die on his behalf, Rondol(046.png) was the first to withdraw his order.

On the third night, a strange old man, Orihalcon(038.png), came instead of Medusa Gorgo(089.png). He claimed to be the Grand Elder of the Iron Palm Mountains and demanded a large sum of money, saying he could break steel with his bare hands and revive a dead person's runes.

Even after being hit by Kadan(041.png)'s Dragon Breaker once, he smiled normally. Celia(062.png) saw a magnetic device tremble beneath his boots. Rather than breaking the steel, it was a trick of cracking it in advance and pulling the pieces with a magnet at the moment of impact.

The assistant behind the scenes was also manipulating the crystal that revived dead runes. Orihalcon(038.png), whose identity was revealed, did not run away. Rather, he unleashed his true hidden power.

Iron Palm's compressed mana exploded continuously through both arms. Kadan(041.png) blocked the first attack, but the second and third impacts exploded inside the shield. Orihalcon(038.png) claimed that he distributed the money he earned through fraud to poor miners.

Celia(062.png) said that whether what he said was true and whether it was right to deceive people are two different things. His habit of putting evil and good deeds on both sides of the scale and erasing each other was stripped away in one sentence.

“Just because you fool people with fake miracles doesn’t mean your power is fake. ” Celia(062.png) shouted to Kadan(041.png). “The impact moves to the other arm.

Don’t hit first, wait for the moment it comes back! ” Kadan(041.png) folded his shield and took a step inside. In the short space where Orihalcon(038.png)'s power passed from his left arm to his right arm, he pushed the Dragon Breaker.

The old man's armor cracked, but Kadan(041.png) only broke his shoulder, avoiding his heart. At that moment, all the lights in the mansion went out. A real visitor, Medusa Gorgo(089.png), came in through the fog.

Using Orihalcon(038.png) as bait, she arrived at Rondol(046.png)'s room and snatched the deportation order from Aeolus(025.png). In the order, under the sentence to capture Medusa Gorgo(089.png) and Belphegor(013.png) alive, there was a secret instruction to bury them with their disciples if it was impossible to retrieve the codex.

Rondol(046.png) did not follow the command and lost his leg. For the first time, Medusa Gorgo(089.png) realized that the person who did not try to kill him was Rondol(046.png). Medusa Gorgo(089.png)'s whip shook as he lost his direction for revenge.

At that time, Belphegor(013.png) led the Poison Dragons and surrounded the mansion. He believed that Medusa Gorgo(089.png) had abandoned him, and he sought revenge on everyone by obtaining the codex. Medusa Gorgo(089.png) returned the welcome stone to Belphegor(013.png).

A scene of two young people and young Celia(062.png) laughing appeared above the fog. Belphegor(013.png) stretched out his hand for a moment, but seeing his past self seemed to forgive his present self, so he broke the illusion stone. Kadan(041.png), Celia(062.png), Rondol(046.png), and Medusa Gorgo(089.png) briefly became on the same side and fought against the Poison Dragons.

The fog circuit bound the enemy's feet, and Medusa Gorgo(089.png)'s chain whip snatched the fan. Kadan(041.png) kept Belphegor(013.png) alive again, but this time he clearly stated the reason.

“It’s not just because you saved my life once. It means that you live to see the end of your choice. ” Belphegor(013.png) laughed and threw himself into the lake.

Medusa Gorgo(089.png) did not follow him. She left, saying she would hear the answer directly from Aeolus(025.png) on Wind Island. After the battle, only broken paintings and water-soaked ledgers remained in the mansion.

Celia(062.png) was afraid to meet his father. Instead of just saying that it would be okay, Kadan(041.png) said that he would be by my side no matter what answer I got. The tide toward Wind Island on the other side of the lake began to change.
