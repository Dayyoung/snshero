Episode 02: Oath of the Seven Pursuers

When Hercul(049.png) arrived at the ruins of Elwin(042.png) village, all that remained were blackened rafters and blood stains on the bridge. He found Aleksandar(050.png)'s broken sword in the snow. The blade had jagged marks that were not found on the official weapons of the Shadow Realm.

It was evidence that someone inside the kingdom had designed the attack. Hercul(049.png) followed the traces into the Seven Bridges of the southern port and ran into seven unexpected people. Blacksmith Grimli(074.png), Crossbowman Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png), Apothecary Eliana(063.png), Blind Stalker Gawain(045.png), Gambler Kaze(044.png), Boatman Rondol(046.png), Acrobat Robinyard(048.png).

They came from different backgrounds and had different accents, and were an unofficial rescue team that took in war orphans. The first meeting wasn't good. Hercul(049.png) believed the report that they were kidnappers, and the seven saw him dressed as a paladin as a stooge of the court.

The market's tent was torn apart and boxes of fish flew through the air. Every time Grimli(074.png)'s hammer hit the guard, golden ripples spread to the bell tower. Gawain(045.png) could not hear Hercul(049.png)'s footsteps, but there was no time to stop his colleagues. Kaze(044.png) scattered seven coins from his pocket to find weak spots in the protective formation, while Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png) selected only the coins that bounced and shot them with his crossbow.

Hercul(049.png) blocked the arrows and turned the angle of his shield to prevent shrapnel from hitting the people in the market. Rondol(046.png) raised the water from the canal and tied Hercul(049.png)'s feet. As he tried to evaporate the water with golden mana, Eliana(063.png) mixed the herb powder.

The hot herbal steam stung my eyes. As the legendary paladin coughed and collapsed on a pile of fish, Robinyard(048.png) couldn't help but laugh even during the fight. It was only one child who stopped the fight.

The child hiding behind Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png)'s back grabbed the hem of Hercul(049.png)'s clothes and said. “Those people didn’t sell us, they saved us from being sold. ” Hercul(049.png) put down his mace.

The seven also put up their weapons, but only Grimli(074.png) snorted instead of apologizing. The ledger of the human trafficking ship they were chasing also had the signature of Lucian(061.png). It was like discovering the same enemy on different paths. The night he was checking the ledgers in the port warehouse, Robinyard(048.png) threw seven liquor jars onto the roof.

[Robinyard(048.png), Grimli(074.png), Lucian(061.png)] The jar did not break, but stopped in the air and spun in a circle. It was not alcohol, but a floating compass that revealed the mana route of the smuggling ship. Each time the blue liquid tilted, a new ray of light appeared on the floor map.

In the ledger, instead of the children's names, their height, weight, and mana attributes were written down. Eliana(063.png) rewrote the name of each rescued child next to it. Hercul(049.png) also silently picked up the pen.

They have been working for a while to return the calculation sheet to human records. One line headed to the northern Wildlands, and the other line headed to the Red Glass Court. Hercul(049.png) noticed that Elwin(042.png) and Ysela(107.png) were pulled in opposite directions.

The two unborn children were also probably somewhere along the way. “I am going to the palace. ” Hercul(049.png) said. “You guys take charge of the north.

” Grimli(074.png) crossed his arms. “Why should we keep your promise instead? ” Hercul(049.png) took out Aleksandar(050.png)'s broken sword and a silver dagger to give to the child.

“It’s not because it’s a request from a dead person. It’s because we can’t just leave a world where living children inherit their parents’ crimes. ” Gawain(045.png) heard the vibration of the dagger with his invisible eyes.

[Gawain(045.png)] A faint but clear resonance of life was answering from the north. He was the first to nod. The remaining six also grumbled or sighed and agreed in their own way.

[Gawain(045.png)] That day, the seven pursuers gathered at the top of the bell tower and made a promise. Whoever finds the child first takes him as his disciple and teaches him the power to protect himself. Eighteen years later, the two children meet under the bell of Seven Bridges, truly ending the tragedy of their parents' generation. Opinions were divided on how to record promises.

Grimli(074.png) was intended to be engraved on a steel plate, and Kaze(044.png) was to be left as a password so as not to be discovered by the enemy. Robinyard(048.png) stamped the handprints of seven people on the inside of the bell. It was a way to ensure that even if one person changed their mind, the others would remember.

Hercul(049.png) added one condition. “Don’t make them each other’s enemies. You can make them compete to see who is stronger, but you shouldn’t make them compete to see who they hate more.

” Before the words were finished, a black arrow pierced the roof of the warehouse. They were court assassins who sensed their pursuit. The seven jars shattered at the same time, spreading a blue curtain, and Hercul(049.png)'s protective guard covered it.

The eight men fought their first joint battle, back to back. At dawn, Hercul(049.png) departed south. The seven pursuers headed toward the northern gateway. On the snow, eight different types of footprints continued side by side for a moment, then split into two.
