Episode 22: Four on a Deserted Island

Only two wounded and two exhausted people remained on the island. Orihalcon(038.png) laughed at Kadan(041.png) who saved him.

He said that since the key was taken away, Leonar(086.png) would die, and since there was no raft, everyone would lie in the same grave. Celia(062.png) did not respond to his taunts and collected the broken wood. The ebony trees that withstood the wind for a long time were lighter than water, and the spider vines inside the island could be used as ropes.

While Kadan(041.png) was carrying logs, she recorded the movements of birds and stars. Kadan(041.png) tried to make the raft square, and Celia(062.png) said that a long shape to cut through the waves would be better. The two tied the tree three times and then untied it.

Leonar(086.png) was lying down and listening to the two people's argument, and muttered that things made together would only last long if both sides showed a reasonable amount of stubbornness. Before Leonar(086.png) lost consciousness, he handed the green jade staff to Celia(062.png). The method of lifting the barrier, known only to the leader of the Free People's League, was also shared.

[Leonar(086.png), Celia(062.png)] “A leader is not someone who saves everyone. It is someone who decides who to save first and does not run away from that decision.

” Celia(062.png) would not accept the cane. Leonar(086.png) did not let go. In the end, when she accepted it with both hands, a wind pattern bloomed on the surface of the green jade.

Three days later the raft was completed. Orihalcon(038.png) crawled alone in the middle of the night and untied the rope. Kadan(041.png) caught him, but the tide dragged the raft away.

Celia(062.png) bent the springy trees on the coast back and made them into giant catapults. “Shoot me. ” When Kadan(041.png) hesitated, she smiled.

“This time it’s my plan, not your recklessness. ” Celia(062.png) flew up from the bent tree and fell into the sea next to the raft.

After diving under the water and loosening a few ropes, he swam back to the island without Orihalcon(038.png) knowing. When the raft went out to sea, it lost its direction and had to ride the current to return. As expected, the next morning, the nearly broken raft and Orihalcon(038.png) washed up on the opposite shore.

He was afraid of Celia(062.png) for the first time. Kadan(041.png) brought him back to life, but this time tied him to the center of the raft. Orihalcon(038.png) could not endure hunger and told a story about the time when he worked at the royal court.

Lucian(061.png) required technicians to record failures before finished products. It was a habit of trying to find out how a device that saves people could kill them. Orihalcon(038.png) took the money and pretended not to know the danger.

Just before departure, two Griffon(084.png) came down from the sky. The birds sent by Titania(064.png) delivered Kadan(041.png)'s letter and searched the sea without returning.

One of them was holding a short scorpion from Ignis(011.png) along with a sealing key. I chased down Death Knight Artus(060.png) and recovered the key. Pay off a debt.

When the key was placed on Leonar(086.png)'s wound, a piece of black soul came out. Kadan(041.png) trapped the pieces with snow crystals, and Celia(062.png) blew them away out to sea with the wind. Leonar(086.png) woke up from a long sleep and the first thing he said was to look for something to eat.

The four people boarded a small raft. Orihalcon(038.png) was a prisoner and Leonar(086.png) could not move. There was only enough drinking water for five days.

Nevertheless, Leonar(086.png) took out alcohol made from fermented coconut and gave everyone a sip. A glass of wine also went to Orihalcon(038.png).

Celia(062.png) was against it, but Leonar(086.png) said that if you do not treat prisoners like human beings, the words you hear during interrogation will not be human words. Even after Orihalcon(038.png) took the glass, he could not put it to his mouth for a while. Celia(062.png) said to Kadan(041.png) that he was still angry.

Kadan(041.png) did not urge him to let go of his anger. The two grabbed the same oar without fully understanding each other's choices. Every time a wave came, the backs of our hands hit each other, and at some point, we started rowing at the same beat, no matter who went first.

On the evening of the third day, lights on land appeared. That was the coast of Elwin(042.png) village, where all the tragedy began. Death Knight Artus(060.png)'s artificial flames were flashing beneath the ruins.
