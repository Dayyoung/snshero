Episode 04: The Child Raised in the Wastes

Six years have passed. Elwin(042.png) raised his son among the tents of Wildland nomads.

The child was strong, but he spoke late and could not properly light a spark of mana. While his peers had fire and wind at their fingertips, Kadan(041.png) carried buckets and mended fences. People called him Kadan(041.png).

This was the name Aleksandar(050.png) chose before he died. Kadan(041.png) thought that who called him was more important than the meaning of his name. One day, Gilgamesh(047.png), a boy in the wilderness, was surrounded by assassins from a hostile tribe.

Kadan(041.png) jumped into the snow without a hunting bow. He threw a chunk of ice to blind the horse and ran towards the frozen river, carrying the fallen Gilgamesh(047.png) on his back. The thin ice cracked, but he spread his weight and crawled out.

Even after arriving across the river, Gilgamesh(047.png) could not speak for a while. He couldn't understand why the child he saw for the first time had fallen into the ice water. Kadan(041.png) took off his wet boots and stood by the fire, saying that if he had been alone, he would have wanted someone to come.

[Gilgamesh(047.png), Kadan(041.png)] “You could still die. ” “That’s why you’re alive.

” Gilgamesh(047.png) never forgot that simple answer for the rest of his life. Gilgamesh(047.png)'s father, Fenrir(081.png), was a war leader who attempted to unite the various tribes of the Wildlands. He offered to give him gold or horses in return for saving his son, but Kadan(041.png) only asked for leather to fix the roof of Elwin(042.png)'s tent.

Fenrir(081.png) laughed out loud and sat Kadan(041.png) down at the family table. There, Kadan(041.png) made a brotherly pledge with Gilgamesh(047.png) and also became close to Fenrir(081.png)'s daughter, Titania(064.png). Titania(064.png) shot a bow better than Kadan(041.png) and was more outspoken.

Every time she smiled, her long red hairband blew in the wind. Titania(064.png) taught letters to Kadan(041.png). Kadan(041.png) missed the middle stroke every time he wrote his name, and Titania(064.png) made him rewrite it dozens of times with a stick over his eyes.

It was because of that winter that Kadan(041.png) later kept his first name tag, which he wrote clumsily, for a long time. “Are you going to go south when you grow up? ” “My mother told me to find the truth.

[Kadan(041.png)] ” “Then promise to return. Anyone who only promises to leave is a coward.

” Kadan(041.png) tied the red string she held out to her wrist. At the time, I didn't know how long a promise could hold a person's heart. Meanwhile, the six pursuers had been wandering the Wildlands for six years.

Without knowing the child's name or face, I followed only the faint resonance of the silver dagger. One night, Gawain(045.png) heard the sound of metal crying on a distant rocky mountain. Kadan(041.png) was pulling out a wolf cub trapped in the ice with his father's dagger.

Grimli(074.png) tested the child as soon as he saw him. He suddenly swung the hammer and broke the ice under his feet. Instead of rushing towards the attacker, Kadan(041.png) first pushed two nearby children to safety.

Even though he fell into the water up to his waist, he did not let go of his dagger. Elwin(042.png) did not believe the six people who suddenly appeared. Even after checking the glass bottle containing Robinyard(048.png)'s ashes and Aleksandar(050.png)'s broken sword, he did not hand over his son.

Every day, Gawain(045.png) sat outside the tent and tapped his cane to Aleksandar(050.png)'s favorite song. A week later, Elwin(042.png) opened the door first and served warm tea.

“You’re slow, you’re dull, and you don’t even know the basics of fighting. ” Grimli(074.png) said. Gawain(045.png) smiled faintly.

“Still, he’s the kid we were looking for. ” The joy of reunion did not last long. That night, in the cave where Kadan(041.png) went to draw water, he heard a person's moaning.

Inside, Belphegor(013.png) was lying in chains. When he saw his pursuers, he laughed and held out his hand to Kadan(041.png) as if he had been waiting for a long time. “Aren’t you curious about the face of the person who killed your father?

” Kadan(041.png) drew the dagger but did not stab. Medusa Gorgo(089.png)'s fingernails flashed in the darkness behind Belphegor(013.png)'s back. The chase, which had not ended for six years, was now catching up with the child's life.
