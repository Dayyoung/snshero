Episode 40: One Who Is Not Called a Hero

When Kadan(041.png) and Celia(062.png) returned to Wildland, the first snow was falling on the grasslands. The Conqueror's huge tent had been pulled down, and Fenrir(081.png) was lying in the small tent that Kadan(041.png) had frequented as a child. Titania(064.png) and Gilgamesh(047.png) stayed by their father's side.

The southern expedition had stopped, but the empire founded by Fenrir(081.png) was already shaken by the succession struggle. His final orders were to send refugees from occupied territories back to their homes and for each tribe to hold a new council. “The land I acquired all my life was scattered in a matter of days.

” Fenrir(081.png) laughed. “Do you think I failed? ” Kadan(041.png) did not give an easy answer. Fenrir(081.png) bound together the tribes that were killing each other and ended the oppression of the Shadow Realm.

[Fenrir(081.png), Kadan(041.png)] At the same time, they tried to take away other people's hometowns for greater unification. Two truths existed together in one person. “You have saved many lives, and you have placed many in fear.

One cannot erase the other. ” Fenrir(081.png) seemed not to like the answer, closed his eyes and asked again. “Then what is a hero? If it is not the person who acquired the largest land or the person who defeated the strongest enemy.

” Kadan(041.png) looked outside the tent. Celia(062.png) was recording the names of war orphans, and Titania(064.png) was distributing food to soldiers who were about to be dispersed going home. Gilgamesh(047.png) sent away the generals who wanted to erect a statue of his father.

“I think he is a person who uses his own power to expand the space for others to live in. Even if his name remains, that space remains. ” Fenrir(081.png) laughed for a while and then coughed. “Then I’d be half hero and half disaster.

” “A person does not end with one name. ” Fenrir(081.png) asked Kadan(041.png) to write his biography honestly. He burned the royal officer's scroll that recorded only his victories, and asked him to leave behind the tribes he killed and the promises he broke.

Titania(064.png) said he would take charge of the record. The Conqueror died that night. I didn't look for the crown until the end, and took Titania(064.png)'s and Gilgamesh(047.png)'s hands on one side each. Kadan(041.png) rejected the generals' requests to take the throne. Celia(062.png) also rejected a proposal to transform the Freemen's League into a kingdom. The two entrusted the Ironforge(080.png) strategic core to archives in several cities, and the Nine Shadows Codex separated healing records and forbidden arts so that anyone could monitor them.

Ignis(011.png)'s glass ember returned all of its power from the Astra Rod, leaving only a transparent piece behind. Kadan(041.png) buried it between the graves of Balian(043.png) and Ysela(107.png). While not disobeying Ignis(011.png)'s last request that he would not lie down next to his parents because he did not qualify, I wanted to leave a place for him to return to if he got lost.
