Episode 28: Revolt of the Jade Council

The Great Council of the Freemen's League was held beneath the Iron Palm Mountains. After Leonar(086.png) stepped down due to injury, it was a place to decide on a new owner for the green jade staff and which side to stand on in the coming war. Thousands of tents filled the valley.

Celia(062.png) did not want to be the leader even if he held a cane. However, the representatives of each region distrusted her because she was an aristocrat of Wind Island. Ignis(011.png) dug into that gap.

He gathered support by distributing food to the refugees and sharing gold from the palace treasury. The gratitude of those who received the food was not false. Ignis(011.png) actually fed a starving child and opened a corrupt warehouse.

The problem was calculating those good deeds in terms of votes and loyalty. He believed in establishing order himself while turning people's gratitude into steps to the throne. Ignis(011.png) did not intend to hand over the federation to Lucian(061.png). Rather, he believed that if he served as both king and chief, no one could abandon the people.

It was the old court logic that if the purpose was right, the power taken away was also justifiable. Kadan(041.png) visited him on the eve of the Congress and returned the wooden horse. Ignis(011.png) saw signs of repairing the wheel and asked if Kadan(041.png) had touched it.

Kadan(041.png) replied that it was a part that Ignis(011.png) fixed himself. I had repaired it without realizing it on a drunken night and couldn't remember it. “Your hands don’t just remember what you break.

” Ignis(011.png) was shaken, but headed to the podium with the wooden horse in his arms. Lucian(061.png) gave him a fake green jade staff and Leonar(086.png)'s fabricated will. Ignis(011.png) went up to the podium even though he knew it was false.

I fooled myself into thinking that once I was called king in front of the crowd, I would have a chance to set the truth straight. Celia(062.png) did not claim forgery in words alone. By sticking a real staff into the ground, he revealed all of the Federation's old escape routes. On each path, the names of the people Leonar(086.png) had personally saved appeared.

The authority of the head came not from the crown, but from the record of lives preserved. Ignis(011.png) tried to cover the record with white flames. Celia(062.png)'s crystal finally shattered.

One refugee collapsed in the flames, and Kadan(041.png) saved him with his shield. Kadan(041.png) and Ignis(011.png) stood face to face for the last time in the center of parliament. Ignis(011.png) shouted that Kadan(041.png) had everything.

Mother's love, teacher, Celia(062.png), people's faith. All he has left is the throne he took from himself. “I’m only looking at what I have, so I can’t see what you threw away.

” Kadan(041.png)'s voice trembled. “We were there for you too. ” Ignis(011.png) unleashed the final rune of the forbidden art. The mana of the people around me was sucked into the white flame.

Kadan(041.png) did not attack, but created hundreds of thin shields through binary division, severing the connection between people and the flame one by one. The runes that had lost their power flowed back. Black cracks started from Ignis(011.png)'s fingernails and spread up his arms and neck.

It was a self-destruct device that Lucian(061.png) had installed from the beginning. Even if he took the throne, he was prevented from coming down alive. Celia(062.png) drew the reverse rune, but Ignis(011.png) had taken too much life.

Kadan(041.png) hugged his friend and tried to connect his core. Ignis(011.png) pushed his hand away. “Don’t save me again and use it as proof that you were right.

” “You don’t need any evidence. You have to live and apologize yourself. ” Ignis(011.png) smiled faintly. I remembered hearing the same thing outside the sealing room.

He sent the remaining flames back to the people. One by one, the extinguished lamps in the valley came to life, and the warmth drained from his body. At the last moment, Ignis(011.png) called Kadan(041.png) brother.

I asked Celia(062.png) not to turn the staff into a crown. And he asked not to bury himself in his parents' grave. The reason was that I had not yet earned the right to lie next to them.

Kadan(041.png) was angry that he was not the one who decided the qualifications. Ignis(011.png) didn't have the strength to answer, so he just smiled with his eyes. One wheel of the wooden horse slowly spun and stopped in the white flames.

The white flame left no ash. Only a small spark that hardened like glass fell on the platform. Kadan(041.png) held it in his hand and could not move for a long time. No one in the crowd shouted the new leader's name.
