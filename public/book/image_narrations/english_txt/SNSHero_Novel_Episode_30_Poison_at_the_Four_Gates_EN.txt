Episode 30: Poison at the Four Gates

“People who are in a hurry tend to stir the water more, but you know how to wait. ” While waiting, Celia(062.png)'s fever rose even more. Kadan(041.png) thought about ignoring the gateway test several times.

When Kadan(041.png) tried to help, the woodcutter refused to help. The problem was not strength, but pulling out the young spirit trapped under the giant tree without harming it. Celia(062.png) announced the position of the wind in a faint voice, and Kadan(041.png) split the binary star to support the weight of each side differently.

After just wetting Celia(062.png)'s lips, I poured the remaining water into the seeds. When the farmer tapped the ground with his foot, groundwater rose and both of them quenched their thirst. [Celia(062.png), Kadan(041.png)] No one said there was only one bottle of water. He first checked the moisture in the field and the underground noise.

The senses taught by the teachers were more useful in moments like this than in fighting. Scholar Sylvania(065.png) waited at the final gate. He asked what was heavier: one life or a thousand people.

Kadan(041.png) did not embellish his answer. “Right now, the one person on my back is the heaviest.

But I will not choose to put a thousand people in danger to save that person. If there is someone who created a situation where I have to compare the two, I will break that false condition first. ” Sylvania(065.png) warned while opening the way.

Aqualord(005.png) was once a king and was able to save a child, but he turned away and abandoned the throne to prevent war. He said that even now he does not meet anyone who asks for healing.

[Malfuon(066.png), Celia(062.png), Kadan(041.png)] The white door of the sanctuary was right in front. The door didn't open.
