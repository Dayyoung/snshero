Episode 10: Prisoner of the Flame Court

Towering at the very core of Lighthaven's inner citadel, the Red Glass Palace projected a bizarre and incandescent majesty found nowhere else on the continent. Constructed of interlocking blocks of crimson obsidian hoisted from volcanic vents and transparent heat-resistant sorcery glass, the corridors hovered directly above bubbling conduits of molten magma. The air was a suffocating cocktail of acrid sulfur and sweet crushed rose perfume, scorching the lungs with every intake of breath, while through the crimson glass floor panels, the silhouettes of subterranean torture cells and labor mines flickered like blood-red phantoms.

High Minister Lucian had convened a grand nocturnal masquerade under the pretext of celebrating the southern campaign. While courtiers adorned in silken robes and glittering gemstones clinked crystal chalices of pomegranate wine with frivolous laughter, Kadan and Cilia slipped past the palace guards disguised among the traveling musicians and banquet attendants. Their objective was to unearth the whereabouts of Balian and the suppressed records of the Arcane Holy Knights.

At the center of the hall, an open circular arena burst into incandescent flames to the roaring cheers of noble spectators. Eighteen-year-old Ignis, commander of the White Flame Order and idolized as the kingdom's youthful champion, was undergoing the ritual Trial of Embers. Clad in ornate white-silver plate, Ignis swung his longsword with immaculate precision, releasing arcs of pure white sanctifying fire that incinerated training beasts into drifting gray ash within single heartbeats.

Nobles rose to their feet in frantic adulation.

"Behold! The pure white fire inherited from Lord Balian himself! The most immaculate blade of the crown stands before us!"

Yet amidst the thunderous acclaim, Ignis's youthful visage remained frozen like carved marble. In his amber eyes gleamed neither pride nor triumph, but a hollow, gnawing desolation.

Seated upon the gilded dais, Lucian raised his golden goblet, a venomous smile curving his lips.

"Magnificent, Ignis. That immaculate blaze upon your blade is the eternal aegis of our crown. Yet true loyalty is proven not by the edge of a sword, but through absolute submission."

At Lucian's gesture, heavy iron winches groaned as a massive slab of red glass in the amphitheater floor slid open. The grim reality of the subterranean dungeon below was laid bare before the court. Suspended over roaring sulfur vents in heavy runic chains was the withered warrior, Lord Balian. His shoulders and bare back were an agonizing canvas of charred lacerations and necrotic brands inflicted by relentless inquisitions.

The tip of Ignis's longsword quivered almost imperceptibly.

"Lord Minister, my father has already been stripped of his knightly mana and reduced to a crippled shell. Why must you subject him to such wanton degradation?"

Lucian leaned forward, whispering with icy satisfaction.

"Until Balian surrenders the ancient Throne Seal of the Holy Order, his agony will find no reprieve. If you fancy yourself a dutiful son, convince the stubborn old hound to speak."

Witnessing this horrific spectacle from the shadow of the gallery colonnade, a tidal wave of fury surged through Kadan's veins. This was Lord Balian—the sworn blood-brother of Master Aleksandar whom he had revered in his mother's bedtime songs—paraded like a broken cur before laughing aristocrats. As Kadan gripped his blade pommel to charge the arena, Cilia's slender fingers clamped around his forearm with iron tenacity.

She hissed urgently into his ear.

"Hold, Kadan! Charging blindly will only trigger Lucian's perimeter wards and see you slaughtered without purpose. I have already memorized the draft patterns of the exhaust shafts. We must wait until the feast winds down and the embers die."

Biting his lip until crimson welled upon his chin, Kadan lowered his shield. Within his clear eyes, the dancing fire of the Red Palace reflected like keen blades.

As the revelry waned into midnight, the two made their way toward a sequestered garden behind the palace. In the midst of the manicured palace grounds stood a solitary, rustic thatched cottage—a jarring anomaly entirely at odds with the court's flamboyant extravagance. Encircled by thorny crimson briar wards and heavily barred windows, a lone woman could be seen steadily turning a weathered wooden spindle, spinning raw flax into rough thread. It was Issela, mother of Ignis and long-lost wife to Lord Balian.

Her hazel eyes were clouded and vacant, bound beneath layers of imperial forgetting curses. Upon her rough-hewn table rested two clay teacups, set out every evening for eighteen long years without her ever recalling whom she awaited. Every evening, she brewed tea that grew cold untouched.

Gathering gentle emerald drafts of wind to deflect the sensory runes surrounding the garden, Cilia whispered.

"Her mind is ensnared within Medusa Gorgo's necrotic amnesia hexes. I shall untangle the magical knots with wind runes, but you must provide the emotional anchor of memory, Kadan."

Stepping softly into the room, Kadan unfastened the silver dagger from his sash, laying it gently upon the wooden table.

"Aunt Issela, do you remember the hamlet of Elwin in the northern reaches? Do you recall the vow sworn beneath the howling blizzard between Uncle Balian and my father, Aleksandar?"

As the names of Balian and Aleksandar resonated through the small chamber, ripples of azure light blossomed from Cilia's fingertips, bathing Issela's brow. With a resonant chime like snapping glass, the suffocating shadow binding her soul dissolved.

Clarity returned to Issela's eyes. Gazing upon the silver dagger and Kadan's earnest countenance, she clutched trembling hands to her lips.

"Ah... Balian... My husband... And my sweet infant torn from my arms in the raging snow...!"

Eighteen years of suppressed agony and maternal yearning burst forth in a torrential cascade of tears. Issela remembered everything: the storm, the ambush, and the ruthless smile of Lucian as her babe was dragged away.

"My precious babe... called my name as he was dragged off in Lucian's cold iron grasp. Every night his cries echoed in my ears, yet under this dark curse I could not even recall whose child wept..."

Kadan clasped her weathered hands in his own, speaking with steady conviction.

"Ignis lives, Aunt Issela. Though deceived by the court's falsehoods and bearing steel against us now, noble blood still flows within his heart. I swear to bring him back to your side."

Suddenly, the cottage doors were violently splintered as Lucian strode over the threshold, flanked by the elite Flame Guard.

A grotesque sneer twisted Lucian's lips as he raised his gilded scepter.

"Rats scurrying in the royal garden. Splendid, Issela. Now that your memories have returned, your value has doubled. Let Balian hear the weeping agony of his beloved wife, and that stubborn hound shall finally surrender the Throne Seal!"

The guards leveled their flaming lances, charging across the floorboards. Without hesitation, Kadan planted his iron shield, projecting the earthen bulwark taught by his masters. With a deafening boom, the flame spears shattered against the shield's surface.

Cilia swept her wind staff high.

"Gale barrier, clear our path!"

A violent tempest erupted within the chamber, hurling stools, earthenware pots, and flax into the guards' faces. Seizing the opening, Kadan hoisted Issela onto his back and broke through the rear timber wall into the garden shadows.

Leaping onto the marble parapets of the upper colonnade, Kadan's gaze locked with a figure standing high on an opposite balcony. It was Ignis, bathed in moonlight. For the first time, Ignis looked upon the tear-streaked face of his mother fleeing below. Beholding the striking resemblance in her eyes, the immaculate blade in Ignis's gauntlet trembled visibly.

"Mother...? Could that woman... be my true mother?"

Ignis's bewildered murmur drifted upon the night wind.

Evading the palace hounds through the subterranean sluiceways, Kadan and Cilia led Issela safely beyond the city walls. Reaching a forest hideout beyond the perimeter, Paladin Walker and the Six Masters stepped forward to welcome them. Upon the windswept hills beneath the starry canopy, Issela took Kadan's hands, her voice choked with emotion.

"Kadan... Save my son Ignis. The boy bears no malice... His soul is merely ensnared within the devil's cruel design."

Kadan tightened his grip upon her weathered hands, looking up toward the constant North Star.

"I swear it, Aunt Issela. I shall break Uncle Balian's chains, and bring Ignis home to your embrace."

Dawn broke over the eastern horizon, scattering the lingering night. Within the breasts of the young heroes, the sacred flame of hope burned brighter than any darkness. Beyond the eastern horizon, a radiant crimson sun arose, illuminating the road of renewed purpose.
