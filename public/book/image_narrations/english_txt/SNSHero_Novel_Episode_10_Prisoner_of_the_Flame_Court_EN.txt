Episode 10: Prisoner of the Flame Court

The red glass palace was as bright at night as during the day. A lava channel flowed inside the ceiling, and flame spirits floated in every hallway instead of lanterns. Kadan(041.png) and Celia(062.png) hung under the medicine cart and entered the villa.

Ysela(107.png) lived alone in the old house at the end of the garden. Thatched roofs, old spinning wheels, and dirt-covered farm tools that did not match the splendid palace were laid out like a piece of home. Lucian(061.png) placed a memory seal on her to make her forget the past, and tricked her into thinking that she was her dead husband's benefactor. Ysela(107.png) turned the potter's wheel at the same time every day and placed two teacups in the same place.

I couldn't remember who I was waiting for. When Kadan(041.png) said Balian(043.png)'s name, his hand first went to the empty teacup. Even though the head was forgotten, the body could not erase the person's place.

When Celia(062.png) lifted the seal, Ysela(107.png) saw the snowstorm from eighteen years ago again. Balian(043.png) falling from a cliff, the black soldiers dragging him, the crying of a newborn baby. She sat down when she heard that Ignis(011.png) was alive. “You think I abandoned that child.

” At the same time, Ignis(011.png) was heading to the villa after receiving orders from Lucian(061.png). It was an order to personally capture the intruders of the pharmacy and prove their loyalty. He heard Kadan(041.png)'s voice and Ysela(107.png)'s crying at the door.

When Ignis(011.png) opened the door, white flames filled the room. Kadan(041.png) spread his shield but did not fight back. Celia(062.png) also took the blow and let Ysela(107.png) step forward. Ysela(107.png) did not retreat even in the flames.

The feeling of carrying Ignis(011.png) out of a tent on fire as a newborn made me move my feet. When she stretched out her hand, Ignis(011.png)'s flames only circled around her finger. “Please don’t come closer.

” Ignis(011.png)'s voice cracked. “Don’t stop if you don’t want to. This time, I don’t want to listen to your wishes and get taken away. ” Ysela(107.png) stopped on the spot.

Instead of running to hug him, I left the option for my son to choose. “You have a crescent-shaped scar on your right shoulder. ” Ysela(107.png) said.

“The fire was so hot when it was born that I put an ice towel on it and the marks were made. ” Ignis(011.png)'s fire wavered. It was a scar that I had never shown to anyone. He took a step forward and then took a step back.

The life he had built up at the court, hating his mother as a traitor his entire life, could not be destroyed with a single sentence. Lucian(061.png) was already watching everything from the roof. He activated the destruction rune hidden in Ysela(107.png)'s memory seal.

As the black flame penetrated her heart, Ignis(011.png) instinctively hugged her mother. The cold of Kadan(041.png) and the flame of Ignis(011.png) enveloped the rune at the same time, and the opposing forces were balanced for a moment. Kadan(041.png) felt that if the ice was even a little strong, Ysela(107.png)'s heart would stop. Ignis(011.png) was also able to burn the memories along with the seal by turning up the heat.

[Ignis(011.png), Kadan(041.png), Ysela(107.png)] The two, unaware of each other's breathing, increased their strength as much as the other person backed away, and immediately lowered it when the other person faltered. The first cooperation was born not of trust but of a common fear of saving one person. “Now you have become a useful brother.

” Lucian(061.png) came down with the palace guards. After hearing that he had plotted his father's death, Kadan(041.png) drew his dagger. However, Lucian(061.png) took Ysela(107.png) hostage and said that Balian(043.png) was still alive. He was a nameless prisoner in a labor prison beneath the palace.

Ignis(011.png) failed to aim his sword at Lucian(061.png). The man raised him as a prince, gave him power and technology, and at the same time took away his biological parents. Neither memory was a lie.

While he was hesitating, security guards poured into the room. Celia(062.png) gathered the wind from the garden and burst the glass wall. Kadan(041.png) carried Ysela(107.png) and ran outside, and the carriage prepared by Walker(051.png) and Eliana(063.png) waited under the fence. Ignis(011.png) received orders to chase, but deliberately set fire to the opposite corridor to block the guard's path.

Lucian(061.png) did not miss the action. “How does it feel to meet your mother? ” “I haven’t yet confirmed if that woman is my mother.

” Ignis(011.png)'s voice was cold, but the flames inside his fist were burning the blood. Ysela(107.png), who left the palace, saw Kadan(041.png)'s dagger and recognized Aleksandar(050.png). She described the last moment when Balian(043.png) pushed her away to avoid Lucian(061.png)'s pursuit. For the first time, the tragedies of two families were connected in one memory.

Inside the carriage, Ysela(107.png) looked at Kadan(041.png)'s face several times. His eyebrows resembled his father's, and his habit of slightly tilting his head when listening to people was similar to Elwin(042.png). Kadan(041.png) pieced together the memories of his father, whom he had never met, from other people's memories.

Kadan(041.png) said he would go back to the dungeon and rescue Balian(043.png). Celia(062.png) did not stop him and unfolded the palace drainage map. Ignis(011.png) was looking at them from the top of the red glass tower in the distance. Between the flame and the snow now lay a path not only toward hatred but toward the same truth.
