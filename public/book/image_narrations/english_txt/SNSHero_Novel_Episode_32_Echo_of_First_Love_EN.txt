Episode 32: Echo of First Love

The place where I found Naiad(002.png) was a small house at the edge of the black swamp. The walls were filled with constellations and water flows calculated over decades, and letters that had never been sent were piled up on the table.

All letters were addressed to Gandar(069.png). When she saw Kadan(041.png) and Celia(062.png), she admitted that she had poisoned them. Knowing that Aqualord(005.png) would lose his strength if he treated Celia(062.png), he deliberately sent two people to the sanctuary.

It was as if Celia(062.png)'s life was put into the calculation for revenge. Naiad(002.png) did not apologize.

Instead of forcing an apology, Aqualord(005.png) accurately told the story of the pain he had experienced and the strength Aqualord(005.png) had lost during the healing process. It was a choice to make people see the human body hidden behind abstract revenge. Celia(062.png) was angry, but before condemning Naiad(002.png), he asked about Aquaris(001.png)'s death.

Naiad(002.png) took out a piece of old cloth. Blood stains remained under the embroidery of two water birds flying side by side. Gandar(069.png) stayed in the sanctuary when he was young and met Naiad(002.png).

He only loved pranks and running away, so he left the kingdom because he was afraid that he was in love with someone. Naiad(002.png) gave birth to Aquaris(001.png) alone, and ran to Aqualord(005.png) holding Aquaris(001.png), who had been injured by Death Knight Artus(060.png)'s soul sword.

Aqualord(005.png) refused on the grounds that if Aquaris(001.png) was saved, the kingdom's ley defenses would be turned off for a day. Thousands of people could die that day. He thought he had made the right decision as king, but after Aquaris(001.png) passed away, he realized that neither life should be left as a number.

“If you realize it, will my Aquaris(001.png) come back? ” Naiad(002.png) asked.

“No. ” Celia(062.png) answered. “So you don’t have to forgive, but if you put someone else in the same calculation, you’re repeating the choices you hated.

” Naiad(002.png) saw a familiar light in Celia(062.png)'s eyes. When I was young, I believed in my own judgment rather than someone else's orders. The years of revenge only dimmed that light.

Gandar(069.png) showed up at home late. After being released from Aeolus(025.png)'s temple, I was looking for Naiad(002.png), but I couldn't come in at the door.

When Kadan(041.png) pushed his back, he fell like a child and entered the room. Gandar(069.png) took out half of the piece of cloth he had received a long time ago. I lied and said I threw it away the day I ran away, but I kept it sewn into my sleeve for decades.

Naiad(002.png) said that keeping records is not evidence of fulfillment of responsibility. For the first time, Gandar(069.png) did not pass it off as a joke and nodded.

Naiad(002.png) neither hit him nor hugged him. The first question was why did you leave? Gandar(069.png) admitted that he ran away not because he loved freedom, but because he was not confident in taking responsibility.

[Gandar(069.png), Naiad(002.png)] Underneath the mask of being a funny old man, there was the most cowardly memory. The two talked all night. The time lost was not made up and my first love did not return to its former self.

When dawn came, Naiad(002.png) handed the pile of letters to Gandar(069.png) without burning them. The responsibility for reading has been returned.

It took Gandar(069.png) an hour to read the first letter. I looked out the window every time as if I wanted to run away, but I didn't leave. Naiad(002.png) made new cups of tea, one cup at a time, until he finished reading.

Something slower and less beautiful than reconciliation began between them. The real reason why Death Knight Artus(060.png) attacked Aquaris(001.png) was also revealed.

Aquaris(001.png)'s bloodline had a rare resonance that stabilized ley lines, and Lucian(061.png) later stole that power to create artificial flames. The forbidden arts implanted in Ignis(011.png) also began with that research. The glass embers throbbed hotly in Kadan(041.png)'s hands.

Ignis(011.png)'s tragedy was planned on top of Aquaris(001.png)'s death even before birth. Naiad(002.png) touched the embers and sang Aquaris(001.png)'s favorite star song. A tiny bit of water flickered once in the flame.
