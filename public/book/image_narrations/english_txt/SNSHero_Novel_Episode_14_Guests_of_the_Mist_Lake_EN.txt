Episode 14: Guests of the Mist Lake

[Kadan(041.png)] The fog lake was as wide as the sea. Instead of the horizon, seventy-two stone island islands supported the clouds, and the towers of an old underwater city were submerged beneath the water.

Kadan(041.png) and Celia(062.png) rented a small boat and entered the center of the lake. A singing voice was heard in the fog. It was a wind lullaby that Celia(062.png) learned from Aeolus(025.png) when he was young.

As she sang the next verse, the one-legged ship owner Rondol(046.png) appeared. He was once a disciple of Aeolus(025.png), but after losing his leg and being driven off the island, he was guarding a refuge for refugees here. Rondol(046.png) did not bow even after recognizing who Celia(062.png) was.

“The child who ran away is back on his feet. ” Celia(062.png) also did not call him teacher. The two took the oars one by one without telling each other why they were angry.

Kadan(041.png) just looked at the waves in the meantime. Rondol(046.png) took the two people to the Cloud Return Mansion. On the wall were hanging mana circuits and ancient maps that looked like paintings.

Kadan(041.png) didn't read much, but he noticed that the strokes of the circuit resembled the movement of a sword. Celia(062.png) laughed as he saw him slashing the air with his fingers in front of the painting. The peace on the lake did not last long.

The Red Glass Court's warship plundered the fishing village under the pretext of paying taxes, and at the head of it was Ignis(011.png), who was chained up. After leaving the palace after the death of his parents, he was captured and was forced to wear a control device that tightened the flame core whenever he refused orders from Lucian(061.png).

Rondol(046.png)'s son, Kaze(044.png), attacked the military ship and took Ignis(011.png) prisoner. The people in the mansion wanted to execute him immediately, but Kadan(041.png) stopped them. Ignis(011.png) refused help and attempted to melt the chains himself.

As the control sphere burned the heart, Celia(062.png) only cut the rune line with the wind knife. In the prisoner's room, Ignis(011.png) asked Kadan(041.png) about his parents' last appearance. When Kadan(041.png) tried to tell the truth, he was immediately stopped.

I asked because I wanted to hear it, but I couldn't bear to hear Kadan(041.png)'s story about a time when I wasn't there. Kadan(041.png) only provided the location of the grave. “You can go later when you’re ready.

There are things there that I don’t need to explain. ” “Don’t pity me. ” Ignis(011.png) said.

“Don’t get me wrong. I saved you because Lucian(061.png) would be happy if you died. ” At Celia(062.png)'s answer, Ignis(011.png) couldn't find anything to say to be more angry.

That night, Belphegor(013.png), a nobleman holding a metal fan, appeared at the mansion. After surviving the rapids, he separated from Medusa Gorgo(089.png) and led the Western Poison Dragon. Every time the fan was opened, poisonous needles and small snake spirits poured out.

He took Ignis(011.png) and tried to use it to decipher Medusa Gorgo(089.png)'s codex. While Kadan(041.png) and Belphegor(013.png) were fighting, Celia(062.png) activated the mansion's picture circuit.

The fog created a different exit for each room, and the Gunseon and Poison Dragons fought each other, mistaking each other for enemies. Rondol(046.png) turned the water wheel on his wheelchair and turned the lake water into a wall. Ignis(011.png) had a chance to escape.

However, when Belphegor(013.png)'s fan aimed at Kadan(041.png)'s back, the stinger burned with white flames. “Your life is a debt I must repay. I will not hand it over to anyone else.

” A poisonous needle stuck in Ignis(011.png)'s shoulder. When Celia(062.png) tried to pull it out, he slapped his hand away, but his knees soon buckled. While Kadan(041.png) held him, Celia(062.png) slowed the flow of poison into the wind.

It was the first moment when the three acted as one body without trusting each other. Belphegor(013.png) retreated and informed Celia(062.png) that Medusa Gorgo(089.png) was heading to Wind Island. Aeolus(025.png) was trying to retrieve the hidden original codex and his own past.

The people in the mansion tried to lock up Ignis(011.png), but Rondol(046.png) released the chains. “Master kicked me off the island, but he couldn’t take away my right to choose. You don’t have to be the person the court decides.

” Ignis(011.png) left for the other side of the lake. Kadan(041.png) and Celia(062.png) didn't know which side he would be on. In the early morning fog, the three boats headed in different directions, but their destination was Whisper of the Wind Island.
