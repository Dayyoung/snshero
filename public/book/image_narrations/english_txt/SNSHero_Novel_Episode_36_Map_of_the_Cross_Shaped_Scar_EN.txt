Episode 36: Map of the Cross-Shaped Scar

There was a cross-shaped crack in the soul storage core that Death Knight Artus(060.png) had abandoned. Celia(062.png) found the same marks on his teachers' weapons.

After making it look like he was dead with Aeolus(025.png)'s flute, the final wound was inflicted with the four-way blade of the soul sword. The half beat of the silver bell left by Gawain(045.png) also served as evidence.

Death Knight Artus(060.png) copied the technique and tone, but could not imitate the moment a living person changed the beat to match the other person's movements. Kadan(041.png) collected the surviving records and went to the garden where his teachers were buried. He knelt in front of Aeolus(025.png).

Instead of asking for forgiveness, he said he had put his anger ahead of the truth. Kadan(041.png) also provided a record detailing the sequence of attacks he made and the injuries suffered by Aeolus(025.png).

This was Celia(062.png)'s advice to not end the apology with emotion, but to leave it as a responsibility necessary for treatment and recovery. Aeolus(025.png) did not wake him up.

“I’m not the only one who accepts your apology. Ask Celia(062.png) too. ” Celia(062.png) held onto the fact that Kadan(041.png) decided to grieve alone and left longer than the fact that Kadan(041.png) did not trust him.

Kadan(041.png) did not try to explain right and wrong. We promised that next time we would not take away the opportunity to grieve together.

[Kadan(041.png)] Their reconciliation did not end with just a hug. After finishing the funeral of our teachers, organizing the list of refugees, and rebuilding our broken house, we continued to have awkward conversations for several days.

The wound did not disappear, but it no longer changed shape in silence. Kadan(041.png) handed Celia(062.png) a nail when he was fixing the roof, and Celia(062.png) corrected a misspelling when Kadan(041.png) wrote down his name. One day, after an argument, we slept in different tents, and the next day we returned to the same workstation.

Long-lasting relationships are built on habits of returning rather than moments of reconciliation. The movement path of Death Knight Artus(060.png) also remained in the storage core.

[Death Knight Artus(060.png)] The four ends of the stigmata of the cross pointed to the Wildlands, Lighthaven Imperial Palace, Iron Palm Mountains, and Astra Peak. By connecting the four ley lines, he could infuse souls into an entire army of dead.

The first target was Wildland. Fenrir(081.png) was already gathering troops to attack the capital of the Shadow Realm. Lucian(061.png) was planning to instigate a war behind the scenes and engulf both sides with his spirit army after the two forces were exhausted.

Kadan(041.png) had to return north. Celia(062.png) said he would lead the Freemen's League and go together.

Aeolus(025.png) entrusted the reconstruction of the island to the residents' council and handed over the mobile core of the sound tower. It was a device that could connect distant units even on the battlefield.

Orihalcon(038.png) asked me to take him too. This was because he felt guilty that the magnetic device that killed his teachers was of his own design. Instead of trying to atone with his life, Celia(062.png) entrusted him with building a mobile barrier.

Orihalcon(038.png) engraved his name in large letters on the first wall he built, but was told by Celia(062.png) to erase it. Instead, he wrote down the names of both the blacksmith and the transport worker who created the device.

The desire to be a hero never went away, but I started learning how to share the credit. Before leaving, Kadan(041.png) took silver bells from each of his teachers' weapons and placed them on the inside of his shield.

Every time I walked, different sounds collided, creating a clumsy harmony. Griffon(084.png) cavalry, led by Gilgamesh(047.png), was waiting at the southern gate. Titania(064.png) said that his father was planning to appoint Kadan(041.png) as Marshal of War.

“Whose promise did you return this time? ” Kadan(041.png) looked at the northern grasslands.

“So that the people where I grew up don’t become instruments of war. ” Titania(064.png) heard the answer and opened the way. A bow with a red string swayed in the north wind.
