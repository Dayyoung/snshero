Episode 36: Map of the Cross-Shaped Scar

Having fought their way through the suffocating illusions of the misty corridors, Kadan and Princess Cilia at last set foot within the highest sanctum of the Black Citadel—the Starlight Archives, where the empire's darkest arcana had lain interred for centuries. The vaulted ceiling of the circular obsidian gallery opened directly to the heavens, where the frigid constellations of the northern sky poured down their pale luminescence upon a colossal granite altar. Chiseled into the altar's stone in deep relief was the Continental Star Chart, tracing every ancient leyline, ridge, and river across the breadth of the realm.

Though the crushing grief of having lost the Six Masters pressed heavily against their shoulders, neither companion faltered in their stride. The rough calluses on Kadan's forearm gripping the Dragon Breaker shield and the crimson cord binding the Silver Dagger whispered of solemn vows sworn in blood, steadying his resolve against the biting night gale.

Through the chilled fog drifting along the flagstones, the gentle warmth of starlight spirits who had watched over these archives for centuries still lingered. Embedded like luminous constellations into the obsidian walls were the archaic cross-shaped stigmata of the holy order, casting radiant amber beams into the shadows. Kadan brushed his boot against the floor's runic engravings, remembering the footsteps of his fallen mentor.

Princess Cilia stepped toward the star chart, raising her jade staff toward the outer mountain ranges.

"This chart is the living lifeline of the realm," Cilia whispered softly. "Though Lucian has warped the subterranean leylines through the foul sorcery of the Nine Shadows Codex, once we shatter the core barrier of the central nexus, the earth's innate purification will restore the mana to its pristine flow."

Kadan scrutinized the central coordinates of Lighthaven and the northern expanse of the Elwin fief.

"Here lies the proof of Lucian's plot to drain the leylines of all seven great sanctuaries through the dark codex. Had our masters not laid down their lives to halt the flow, the continent would have shattered beneath our feet long ago."

As Kadan pressed his gauntleted hand upon the coordinate of Lighthaven, a metallic click echoed through the altar base, and a concealed stone coffer rose smoothly from the floor. Within lay a weathered, blood-crusted leather pouch enclosing an ancient parchment tactical diagram etched with a deep cross-shaped scar.

It was the Map of the Cross-Shaped Scar, drafted twenty winters ago during the First Crusade by Master Aleksandar and Uncle Balian, tracing the sacred stigmata carved across their own chests.

With trembling fingers, Kadan unfurled the yellowed parchment. Upon it, Master Aleksandar's brushstrokes remained as sharp and vivid as iron.

'To my child Kadan. If your eyes read these words, I have already returned to the earth. Yet weep not. Though a paladin's shield must turn to dust, the seed of life shielded beneath its iron shall take root and blossom upon this soil. Lucian's dark wards appear impenetrable, yet when the living warmth of the people dwelling in the four cardinal sanctuaries unites, the dark citadel's core shall crumble like sand.'

The brushwork was rugged, yet every stroke brimmed with boundless faith and tender fatherly love. Foreseeing the agonizing trials the orphaned boy would endure in his absence, Aleksandar had inscribed this testament of hope in his own blood until his final breath.

Kadan touched the worn parchment edge, moisture welling in his eyes.

"Master never feared his own demise. His only thought was the brightness of the dawn awaiting the children who walked behind him."

Cilia drew near, clasping his weathered hand, and decoded the four cardinal runes inscribed along the margin.

"The northern snow, the oceanic gale, the imperial sacred flame, and the guardian shield of the earth... The intersection where these four primordial forces converge is the Throne of Ruin where Lucian awaits. Master left us the ultimate key to victory."

Kadan retrieved Ignis's White Glass Ember from his tunic and nestled it upon the sacred flame node of the parchment. In an instant, pure white sanctifying flames surged from the glass vessel, racing along the scarred contours of the map to envelop the obsidian dais in ripples of incandescent gold and radiant jade. The flame Ignis had bequeathed at the cost of his mortal life fused seamlessly with Master Aleksandar's tactical diagram, manifesting a luminous miracle that banished the darkness.

Clenching his fist, Kadan looked upon the light.

"Ignis breathes alongside us even now. The three flames and the sacred shield are finally united."

Cilia nodded, aligning her jade staff with the wind node.

"All the solemn oaths of the ancient heroes have gathered at our fingertips."

Kadan unsheathed the Silver Dagger, resting its tip at the map's nexus.

"Master Aleksandar, Uncle Balian, and Ignis stand with us. We have no time to linger in grief."

Suddenly, thunderous hoofbeats and the resonant roars of great wolves pierced the nocturnal air from the abyss beneath the terrace. King Fenrir's Silver Wolf Vanguard had enveloped the lower bastion of the Black Citadel, launching a fierce assault against the demonic legions.

The battle cries echoing from the lower terraces reignited the spirits of the allied hosts. King Fenrir and his northern warriors methodically dismantled Lucian's reserve beast legions, severing every outer line of communication and reinforcing the perimeter. Because the northern beasts held the bloodstained sulfur swamps, Kadan and Cilia were freed from guarding their vanguard, able to focus their entire will upon the tyrant on the throne.

Kadan raised his Dragon Breaker shield toward the warriors battling below.

"King Fenrir's loyalty has lifted the burden from our shoulders. Now it is our turn to cleave the tyrant on the throne."

Securing the parchment and the glowing glass ember, the two companions advanced toward the colossal obsidian portal sealing the inner sanctum. From beyond the threshold drifted the choking stench of brimstone and the violent ripples of corrupted mana.

Standing before the stone gate, Kadan hoisted his shield to his chest, its cross-shaped insignia humming in resonance with the parchment's scar.

Cilia gripped her jade staff with both hands, standing shoulder-to-shoulder with him.

"Lucian waits beyond this threshold. Whatever darkness lurks within, I shall not yield half a pace from your side."

Kadan offered a warm, unwavering smile.

"The warmth of the lives we must protect rests at our backs. Come, Cilia. Let us open the final door to wipe away every tear from this land."

Kadan drove his shield into the portal's center, shattering the massive stone gates in a roar of splintering granite. Beyond lay the blood-drenched Throne of Ruin, and the monstrous silhouette of High Chancellor Lucian waiting in the dark.
