Episode 39: The World Worth Protecting

As the Three Silk Seals shattered and the shroud of the Nine Shadows Codex dissipated, a crisp morning breeze swept through the ruined colonnade. Yet from the shadows behind the splintered dais, a battered figure in dented black armor dragged himself forward—Sir Artus, the final Death Knight.

Artus's crimson eyes were dim, and his fractured obsidian broadsword dripped necrotic black blood. Only the residual tendrils of Lucian's puppetry compelled his withered frame to advance.

Through parched lips, the fallen knight rasped:

"A paladin's vow... is never sullied... even in the dark..."

Kadan lowered his Dragon Breaker shield and pointed the Silver Dagger toward the floor.

"Sir Artus. You were once a peerless champion of the holy knights and the dearest friend of Uncle Balian. The time has come to cast off these cursed chains and enter eternal rest."

Cilia raised her jade staff, letting a gentle melody of wind drift across the shattered stones.

"His soul weeps in agony. Though his body was made a monster, the heart of a paladin beats within, yearning for release."

Artus brought down his broken broadsword with a desperate cry—not an attack of malice, but a plea for deliverance. Kadan did not draw his steel; instead, he stepped forward, meeting the blow with the gentle embrace of his shield.

A soothing aura of golden starlight radiated from the shield face—Master Aleksandar's ultimate art, 'Star Deliverance.' The necrotic tendrils wrapping Artus's plate disintegrated into luminous white butterflies that drifted upward into the sky. His battered helm slipped away, revealing the serene countenance of a white-haired elder.

Kneeling before Kadan, Artus took the youth's hand with trembling fingers.

"Thank you... child of Aleksandar... Your shield has redeemed me... Now I may face Sir Balian and Sir Aleksandar with head held high... This world... was truly worth defending..."

With a peaceful smile, the old knight's spirit dissolved into silver dust, carried away on the morning breeze.

Only Lucian remained, weeping black bile upon the marble floor. Stripped of his codex, the once-mighty tyrant had withered into a frail, trembling old wretch. He clawed at Kadan's boots, begging for mercy.

"Spare me, Kadan! I shall grant you every treasure and province in the empire! Only spare my life!"

Kadan looked down with eyes as cool as northern mountain streams.

"The villages you burned, the captives who perished in the mines, and the lives of my masters and Ignis cannot be bought with gold. You shall answer for your deeds before the judgment of law and history."

Allied soldiers bound Lucian in heavy chains and hauled him down to the dungeons. The eighteen-year tyranny that had choked the realm had come to an end.

Kadan and Cilia stood side-by-side upon the terrace. Below, the dark storm clouds parted as the morning sun rose over the eastern sea, bathing the scarred land in gold. The sulfur fog was swept away, revealing green forests and sparkling rivers.

Cilia leaned against Kadan's shoulder, tears shimmering in her eyes.

"We have done it, Kadan. True dawn has arrived."

Kadan retrieved Ignis's White Glass Ember, holding it toward the sun. The vessel emanated a steady, warm white light, as though their fallen brother were smiling beside them.

"Are you watching, Ignis? Master, uncle... We have kept our vow."

Far below, tens of thousands of allied soldiers and liberated citizens raised their weapons, sending a thunderous cheer across the mountains. Cilia swept her staff, releasing a blessing of gentle wind across the valleys.

"This world bears countless scars, but the warmth of hands held makes it beautiful and worth defending."

Kadan lowered his shield, clasping her hand tightly.

"To protect that warmth, we raised our shield. Now let the laughter of the people fill this earth."
