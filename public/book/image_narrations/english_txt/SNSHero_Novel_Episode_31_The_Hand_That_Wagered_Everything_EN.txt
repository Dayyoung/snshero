Episode 31: The Hand That Wagered Everything

Aqualord(005.png) placed his hand on Celia(062.png)'s chest and examined the broken wind core. He said he could be saved, but at a high cost.

If you transfer half of your life mana, you will not be able to use healing arts for at least five years, and if enemies from long ago come to visit you, you will lose the power to protect the sanctuary. Kadan(041.png) said he would give up mana instead.

Aqualord(005.png) shook his head. Even though Kadan(041.png)'s frost core could suture wounds, it could not revive the beat of the wind.

Aqualord(005.png) had Kadan(041.png) watch the healing. It meant breaking the habit of saying that you would sacrifice yourself to avoid seeing your loved one suffer. Every time Kadan(041.png)'s fingers trembled, Kadan(041.png) stood up and sat down again.

[Aqualord(005.png), Kadan(041.png)] “Paying the price for a choice is not always a sacrifice. Sometimes it also means taking away the other person’s right to decide.

” Aqualord(005.png) woke up Celia(062.png), who was faintly conscious, and asked him directly. After surviving, will he return to the Freemen's League's war and his own responsibilities, or will he stop here without suffering?

Celia(062.png) barely moved his lips. “I haven’t been able to return the wand yet. ” The healing began at sunset and lasted until noon the next day.

From Aqualord(005.png)'s fingertips, a watery pattern flowed along Celia(062.png)'s veins, while Kadan(041.png) held onto the ley line with the four gate guardians outside. In Celia(062.png)'s memories, the wind swirled around the sanctuary.

The smell of bread from Janggagu Market, the song of Sylphid(023.png) heard on Wind Island, and the hot ashes left by Ignis(011.png) shook the doors and windows one after another. Aqualord(005.png) did not cut out any memories and let the core find its own beat.

The moment Celia(062.png)'s core started beating again, Aqualord(005.png) vomited blood and collapsed. His hair became as white as snow. As soon as Celia(062.png) opened his eyes, he realized that a person had lost years of life because of him.

“Don’t end with just saying thank you. ” Aqualord(005.png) smiled.

[Aqualord(005.png)] “If you want to make my choice valuable, next time you ask and listen to someone else’s choice. ” That night, poison was found in a vial in the sanctuary.

It was placed by a woman named Naiad(002.png) in the pocket of Sylvania(065.png), who guided the four gateways. The poison was not aimed at Celia(062.png), but at Aqualord(005.png), which had completed healing.

Naiad(002.png) was a court mage who read the stars in the kingdom of Aqualord(005.png) a long time ago. She fell in love with Gandar(069.png) and gave birth to Aquaris(001.png), but Aqualord(005.png), fearing for the kingdom's reputation, separated them. Even when Aquaris(001.png) was mortally wounded in an attack by Death Knight Artus(060.png), he refused to be healed for fear of breaking his neutrality in the war.

Even after Aqualord(005.png) drank the poison, he did not look for an antidote first. It was only when he heard that Naiad(002.png) had used Celia(062.png)'s life to lure him here that his face shook.

He said she needed to know that her revenge had made others the same victims. Aqualord(005.png) made no excuses.

In the name of protecting a country, he ignored Aquaris(001.png)'s life, and from that day on, he abandoned the throne. Naiad(002.png) believed that punishing himself was not enough. Celia(062.png) separated the components of the poison with wind.

While saving Aqualord(005.png), it left behind a scent that could trace Naiad(002.png)'s whereabouts. Kadan(041.png) said that the cycle of revenge must be prevented from starting again.

Aqualord(005.png) looked at Ignis(011.png)'s spark and found out that other life mana was mixed within it. Some of the power that Ignis(011.png) sent back at the end could not find its owner and remained in the embers.

That power was later able to hold the core of a dying person once. When Kadan(041.png) heard those words, he squeezed the embers harder. The fact that I had to decide when and on whom to use the power left behind after my friend died added another layer of weight.
