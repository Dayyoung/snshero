Episode 21: Betrayal Aboard the Burning Ship

The footprints on the beach belonged to Leonar(086.png). He rescued a refuge ship and searched for Kadan(041.png) and Celia(062.png), but was pushed by a storm and ended up on the same island.

There was a deep black wound on one shoulder. It was a trace of a fight with Death Knight Artus(060.png). Leonar(086.png) climbed onto Lucian(061.png)'s command ship to rescue refugees, but fell into a trap.

Death Knight Artus(060.png) was an undead knight with no body inside his armor. After channeling the opponent's attack into the empty armor, it left a direct wound on the soul. Even the barrier of the green jade staff could not completely block his sword.

Leonar(086.png) hid his wounds as if he were bragging. When Celia(062.png) tried to cut the armor, he found the meat, saying it was fine, and collapsed after one step. As Kadan(041.png) carried him to the cave on his back, he realized that he too had behaved the same way in the face of danger.

Celia(062.png) glared at the two people alternately and asked, “Is the common technique of strong men hindering treatment? ” The three people made their home in a coastal cave. Celia(062.png) washed the wound with herbs and sea salt, and Kadan(041.png) collected rainwater and dug an ice cellar.

It was peaceful during the day, but every night the sound of metal dragging in the forest grew closer. Death Knight Artus(060.png) was on the island with Orihalcon(038.png). They targeted Kadan(041.png)'s codex pieces.

Orihalcon(038.png) lied and offered a deal, saying he had seen a rescue ship. The condition was that he would take everyone with him if he read the codex. When Kadan(041.png) refused, Death Knight Artus(060.png) awakened the soul fragment planted in Leonar(086.png)'s wound.

A black crack spread to my heart. To save Leonar(086.png), the sealing key held by Death Knight Artus(060.png) was needed. Kadan(041.png) bought time by pretending to transcribe the codex.

He arranged the sentences in reverse order but hid minor errors so as not to overwhelm them immediately after reading them. Celia(062.png) adjusted the drying speed of the ink with the wind to create different densities between real and fake runes. Kadan(041.png)'s handwriting was slow, so he almost got caught cheating.

Celia(062.png) deliberately argued with Orihalcon(038.png) to attract attention. Every time he boasted about his wisdom, he asked a more difficult question and kept people talking all night long. Buying time by fueling the enemy's vanity was a battle that Celia(062.png) could not imitate.

Orihalcon(038.png) pushed Kadan(041.png)'s raft into the sea at night to leave Celia(062.png) alone. It was a twisted calculation that if he was trapped on the island forever, he would rely on himself.

When he returned, Celia(062.png) pretended not to know anything and asked about the rescue boat. She lured Orihalcon(038.png) into a trap below the cliff. A pocket of wind hidden between the rocks burst and the ground collapsed, and a huge stone crushed his legs.

The tide began to rise. “Leave that person behind. ” Celia(062.png) said.

“He destroyed the raft. He is with the same people who killed Leonar(086.png). ” Kadan(041.png) said he would save Orihalcon(038.png).

Celia(062.png)'s face stiffened. “Does your goodness always come before our lives? ” The tide rose even as the two men argued.

Celia(062.png) did not wait for Kadan(041.png) to find the right thing to say. I got angry as I hung a rope on a rock and calculated the rescue angle. Kadan(041.png) felt even more sorry because he was a person who could separate emotions from what needed to be done.

Kadan(041.png) could not answer that question right away. Behind my conviction that I was doing the right thing, there were moments when I refused to see the price paid by those close to me. He said, holding Celia(062.png)'s hand.

[Kadan(041.png), Celia(062.png)] “I will not take lightly the reason you are against it. Still, if you abandon someone who could have been saved right before my eyes, I might blame you for that choice for the rest of my life. I don’t want to be that person.

” Celia(062.png) looked at the cracks in the rock without losing his anger. The two used Leonar(086.png)'s staff as a lever and pushed snow and wind into the gap to lift the rock. Orihalcon(038.png) survived, but had difficulty regaining use of his legs.

Meanwhile, Death Knight Artus(060.png) left the island with a copy of the codex and the sealing key. Leonar(086.png)'s breathing became shallow. On the horizon was a single burning sail receding away.
