Episode 21: Traces of the Burned Ship

When the armada of hundreds of flower boats led by the flagship 'Breeze Wing' crossed the treacherous ocean swells to reach the southwestern coast of the mainland, what welcomed the companions was not fluttering banners of celebration, but the suffocating stench of burning pitch and towering pillars of blackened smoke. The timber wharves of Seven Bridges Haven, once a tranquil maritime sanctuary connecting the northern tundra with the eastern seas, had been reduced to smoldering ash, while receding tides dragged fractured spars and scorched decking across the mudflats. In this desolate graveyard where not even a solitary gull cried, only the bitter reek of scorched cedar bore testimony to the scale of the atrocity. Rusted harbor chains lay snapped amidst the tidal mud, and the timber warehouses along the shoreline stood as skeletal frames vomiting black soot into the gray sky.

Gripping the helm, Rondol surveyed the ruined piers, his broad fists trembling with suppressed rage.

"Merciless curs... To trap the fleeing populace, they incinerated civilian transports and fishing cutters without distinction. A decade ago, this haven bustled with grain barges and silk merchants from every corner of the world, yet the madness of those blinded by power knows no boundary."

Bracing his unadorned iron shield, Kadan leaped first onto the wet mudflats, his leather boots crunching through crushed seashells and charcoal dust.

"Lucian's imperial vanguard has fortified the coastline and initiated a brutal scorched-earth campaign. The fires were set less than half a sunrise ago. We must search for survivors amidst the ruins."

Raising her Jade Staff to gather the coastal winds, Cilia knit her brow in deep concentration.

"Beneath the western sea cliffs near the salt cellar, I sense the faint, labored breathing of living souls. But lethal mana snares are woven densely across the approach."

Weaving through heaps of sodden ash and charred timber ribs, the companions advanced cautiously toward the western cliff face. There lay the canted keel of a massive, burned hospital barge, dark bloodstains smeared along the fractured hull. Medicine chests and linen bandages lay trampled into the muck, and upon a broken tea bowl rested the faint, weathered cross-crest of the Arcane Holy Knights. It had been a sanctuary of mercy established decades ago by Master Aleksandar and Uncle Balian to relieve northern famines.

Smoothing the scarlet ribbon bound to his silver dagger, Kadan spoke in a hushed, reverent murmur.

"This was the charitable clinic established by Uncle Balian and the Holy Knights. A haven that healed impoverished fishermen and orphans... incinerated into cinder."

Just then, a weathered trapdoor at the base of the granite cliff creaked open, and a white-haired elder brandishing a broken stone pestle peeked out with trembling wariness. It was Ordo, the elderly apothecary of the haven.

Beholding the sacred cross-crest upon Kadan's iron shield, Ordo broke into racking sobs of relief.

"Praise be to the heavens! The sacred shield of the Holy Knights has returned! Lucian's Crimson Wolf vanguard put the harbor to the torch and dragged our able-bodied youths to the labor mines, but I managed to conceal twenty orphaned children beneath these salt vaults!"

Stepping forward, Ignis took the old man's trembling hands, bowing his head in solemn remorse.

"Forgive our tardy arrival, elder. For the atrocities committed by the imperial host, I offer my life in earnest atonement."

Cilia lifted a broken ceramic herb jar, tears glittering upon her lashes.

"To set fire to a sanctuary dedicated solely to mercy and healing is an unforgivable sacrilege."

Descending into the salt cellar, the companions beheld twenty orphaned refugee children and wounded fishermen huddled together amidst sacks of coarse sea salt. Caked in grime and starving after days without rations, the children shivered, while injured mariners clutched gangrenous limbs in silent agony. The damp subterranean chill froze the little ones' bare feet blue.

Without a second thought, Cilia knelt upon the damp stone, extracting soothing restorative powders from her satchel to dress their lacerations.

"Do not fear. You are safe now. This is the soothing balm of mountain herbs brought from Whispering Wind Island."

As gentle teal mana radiated from Cilia's staff, stroking the children's fevered brows, their rasping breath smoothed into peaceful cadence.

Kadan drew loaves of barley bread and dried venison from his pack, placing them into the children's fragile hands.

"Eat slowly and take heart. Beyond these shores, hundreds of festive flower boats are waiting to bear you to safety."

A small girl touched Kadan's battered iron shield with her tiny fingers, weeping softly.

"Mister knight... The men in armor set fire to our home... Where has my mother gone?"

Caressing the girl's hair with tender gentleness, Kadan mastered the blazing fury surging in his breast.

"I will find your mother, little one. And those who crushed your hearth shall answer for every drop of sorrow they caused."

Offering his canteen of honeyed water to the injured elders, Ignis spoke with earnest depth.

"Moisten your throats. I swear upon my life to hold this battle line so the hunting hounds of the court can never harm you again."

In that tender moment, the sharp crunch of armored boots and the rhythmic creak of cocked crossbows echoed outside. Thirty elite inquisitors of Lucian's guard, alerted by the smoke, had cordoned off the cellar entrance.

Kicking down the outer door, the inquisition commander sneered in cruel triumph.

"So the vermin survived beneath the stones! Exterminate the traitors and feed their bones to the sea!"

Without hesitation, Kadan charged through the threshold, raising his iron shield. A volley of poisoned bolts shattered against the curved surface with deafening clangs.

"Not a single foot shall approach these children!"

Channeling star-breathing, Kadan spun his shield, snapping spearheads and shattering the vanguard's formation. Ignis charged beside him, his iron weapon wreathed in roaring white fire. The sanctified heat incinerated the assassins' plate armor, driving them headlong into the muck, while Cilia's blinding sandstorm stripped away their vision.

Sprawling in the mud, the inquisition commander hurled away his shattered helmet, shrieking in venomous rage.

"Ignis! Have you truly renounced the grace of the throne to become a lackey for northern rebels! Minister Lucian has placed the empire's greatest bounty upon your head!"

Planting his weapon into the earth, Ignis glared down with frigid disdain.

"Lucian's 'grace' is merely a sandcastle built on the blood of the innocent. Return and tell him: the shield of the Holy Knights and the flame of redemption will soon arrive to shatter his gilded throne!"

With the threat vanquished, tranquil surf returned to the shore.

Kadan and Ignis escorted the refugees safely onto the flower boats, where Rondol's crew served steaming carp soup to the shivering children. Beyond the western horizon, the rose-gold dusk cast long, dramatic shadows across the ruined harbor. From the decks of the flower boats drifted the tender, solemn melodies of seafarers' hymns.

Touching the scorched stone of the lighthouse, Cilia spoke with solemn resolve.

"The entire continent groans beneath Lucian's reign of terror. The road ahead will test every fiber of our spirits."

Ignis clenched his fists with unyielding conviction.

"They burned timber and stone, but they could never incinerate the hope of the people. At every crossroads toward the capital, we will deliver the suffering from despair."

Gazing toward the distant western mountain passes, Kadan tightened his grip on his dagger.

"This is the realm Master Aleksandar and Uncle Balian bled to protect. Even in the ashes of despair, we have defended the warmth of living souls. Now we raise our shields and walk that road until the dawn."

Under the dying light of dusk, the three youths sealed their unbreakable oath, taking their first bold strides into the heart of the troubled continent. Beyond the cold night fog, the rugged peaks of Mount Dragonhorn silently watched over their march.
