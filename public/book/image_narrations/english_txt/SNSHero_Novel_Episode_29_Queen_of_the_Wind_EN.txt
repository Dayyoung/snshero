Episode 29: Queen of the Wind

The day after Ignis(011.png)'s death, the Freedmen's League held another congress. Celia(062.png) did not prepare black robes or a memorial speech.

[Ignis(011.png), Celia(062.png)] Instead, I started by reading the names of those who had been injured the day before, the medicine needed, and the amount of food lost. The representatives entrusted the position of leader to her, who held a green jade staff.

Celia(062.png) put a condition on it. The conditions were that the head would not be inherited by blood, that he would step down immediately if three or more regions voted against him, and that an evacuation plan must be attached to every wartime order. Representatives objected, saying it would weaken their authority too much.

Celia(062.png) reminded Ignis(011.png) that an organization where one person can make decisions quickly put Ignis(011.png) on the stage. Both the costs of slow agreement and the costs of fast dictatorship had to be put on the books.

[Celia(062.png), Ignis(011.png)] “Don’t call me queen, because the wind doesn’t sit on the throne. ” Nevertheless, people called her the Queen of the Winds. A name mixed with respect and expectations was sometimes heavier than shackles.

The first mission was to find traitors within the Federation. Lucian(061.png)'s gold was flowing to various representatives, and fake orders were leading refugees into a dead-end valley in the Iron Palm Mountains.

Celia(062.png) took off his chief's attire and put on the clothes of a beggar from Zhangjiagou Market again. Kadan(041.png) pretended not to recognize her, just like when they first met, but he couldn't hide his smile.

The two joined the refugee procession and chased after the fake guides. The traitors spread a rumor that Kadan(041.png) killed Ignis(011.png). Angry refugees surrounded Kadan(041.png).

He did not draw his sword and told exactly what he had done in the last battle. I stopped Ignis(011.png), tried to save him, and failed.

One woman said her child survived on food released by Ignis(011.png). Another man lost his brother to Prohibition. Kadan(041.png) did not refute any of the memories.

Loving or hating a person did not erase the other consequences he left behind. Instead of defending Kadan(041.png), Celia(062.png) showed the field record crystal ball.

[Kadan(041.png), Celia(062.png)] The right to judge the facts was returned to the crowd. After being silent for a long time, people opened the way.

The traitors' home base was an abandoned smelter midway up the mountain range. A fake elder claiming to be Orihalcon(038.png)'s older brother gathered followers by demonstrating the miracle of melting steel with his bare hands. Celia(062.png) found a firestone pipe hidden under the heat source.

When his identity was revealed, the fake Grand Elder used the real Iron Palm shock technique. His compression wave hit Celia(062.png)'s back, shattering his windscale armor from the inside.

Even as she fell, she opened an escape passage with her green jade staff. Kadan(041.png) did not chase the enemy, but carried Celia(062.png). If he were his old self, he would have tried not to miss the villain.

Now I know that it is my responsibility to decide who to save first and deal with the consequences. Before Celia(062.png) lost consciousness, he ordered the fake Grand Elder to be let go.

Kadan(041.png) also told me not to pretend to be my own judgement and to leave a record of following orders. This was meant to ensure that the path of responsibility does not disappear even if the leader falls.

Celia(062.png) stole the map from the fake Grand Elder's waist before losing consciousness. The map showed four gateways across the mountain range and the sanctuary of Aqualord(005.png) hidden at the end of them. He who manipulated life mana could repair the broken wind core.

Kadan(041.png) tied the green jade staff to his back and picked up Celia(062.png). Federation representatives followed suit, saying they would protect the leader, but he only allowed the minimum number of people.

A large army only irritated the gateway. That night, Ignis(011.png)'s glass spark shined once in Kadan(041.png)'s pocket. A thin flame tilted toward the south, as if indicating the remaining path.
