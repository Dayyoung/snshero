Episode 19: The Three Trials

Ascending the final coils of the spiraling staircase, the companions reached the Sky Altar, suspended thousands of fathoms above Whispering Wind Island. Far below stretched the panoramic expanse of the azure ocean and jagged coastal shoals, while overhead, the colossal mana vortex of the Jade Barrier Spire cleaved through the thunderheads with concussive roars. Across a narrow stone bridge bridging the chasm stood King Aeolus, once revered as the sovereign Spirit King of the Winds. His silver hair whipped wildly in the tempest, his eyes clouded with profound grief and corrupted by High Elder Zephyr's phantom illusions. Crackling ribbons of lightning coiled around the massive broadsword held in his trembling grasp.

Leveling his storm blade, Aeolus bellowed in a voice hoarse with pain.

"None shall cross this bridge! I will never permit the heir of the treacherous Holy Knights who butchered my beloved Sylphid, nor the wayward princess who consorts with our foes, to desecrate the sanctuary!"

Stepping forward with the Jade Staff, Cilia pleaded through falling tears.

"Father, look upon me! Look at mother's staff in my hands and this living memory crystal! It was not the Holy Knights who took mother's life, but High Elder Zephyr, who poisoned her cup in treasonous collusion with the imperial court!"

Clutching his temples in agony, Aeolus refused to lower his guard.

"Vile deceptions! Zephyr testified before the council that the silver dagger of the Holy Order pierced my queen's heart! If the youth beside you is truly innocent, let him brave my three trials to prove his purity of purpose!"

Planting his boots firmly, Kadan raised his unadorned iron shield to chest height.

"I will not shrink from any trial. If crossing this bridge can vindicate Master Aleksandar and prove Cilia's truth, I wager my life upon this stone."

Ignis gripped Kadan's shoulder with solemn urgency.

"Stay sharp, Kadan. King Aeolus's storm blade is a divine relic of the winds, capable of cleaving imperial knights in twain with a single stroke."

The first trial was the Trial of the Gale.

With a sweeping gesture from Aeolus, thousands of razor-sharp wind blades erupted across the bridge, churning the air with deafening shrieks. The kinetic pressure was fierce enough to pulverize granite, while beneath the narrow span lay an abyss plunging straight into the ocean depths. A glancing gale sheared Kadan's cheek, drawing crimson blood.

Yet Kadan drew no weapon. Bracing his unadorned iron shield, he drew star-breathing deep into his lungs.

"Master Aleksandar taught me never to fight the gale, but to embrace its flow through the unyielding weight of the earth."

Angling the face of his shield at forty-five degrees, Kadan deflected the tempest's fury into the empty air. Wind blades shrieked violently against the iron rim, yet Kadan advanced step by steady step across the stones. Blood seeped through his sleeves where veins strained under the pressure, yet his eyes remained as tranquil as a frozen northern tarn. He pressed forward like an immovable iron monolith cleaving the storm.

Halting midway across the bridge, Kadan stood firm, shaking Aeolus to his soul.

"To advance without unsheathing steel, taking the storm upon his own flesh... That grounded posture and that impenetrable guard... It is beyond question the peerless art of Aleksandar."

The second trial was the Trial of Memory.

The moment Kadan set foot on the center of the span, Aeolus's arcane illusion erupted, transfiguring the altar into the flaming ruins of the Elwin estate in the north. Before Kadan's eyes rose the slaughter of his childhood: village elders cut down and his parents' dying cries echoing through the smoke. From the shadows coiling around the stones hissed a serpentine voice: 'Hate this corrupt world. Draw your blade and burn the empire and the wind kingdom in retribution. Avenge the blood stolen from you.'

Violent fury and grief constricted Kadan's throat, tempting him to draw his steel and butcher the phantoms.

Yet Kadan gripped the scarlet ribbon bound to his silver dagger, breathing out a steady, measured breath.

"My shield was never forged for bloody vengeance. It exists to protect the fragile warmth of those shivering behind me, to halt the cycle of tragedy forever!"

With a decisive thrust of his shield, Kadan shattered the phantom mist. The dark mirage fractured like brittle glass, returning the pristine light of the Sky Altar. Staring at the young warrior who refused to surrender to hatred, Aeolus fell speechless.

"He broke the nightmare not through wrath... but solely through the resolve to protect..."

The final trial was the Trial of Sacrifice.

As Aeolus invoked the central lightning glyphs, a colossal thunderbolt formed in the clouds, crackling with righteous execution. It was a lethal judgment designed to cease only when an intruder's life was consumed.

In that fatal instant, Ignis vaulted across the stones, throwing his unarmored body beside Kadan. Conjuring his immaculate white flame, he erected a radiant dome of pure fire.

"I will not let my brother perish alone! If blood must atone for imperial crimes, let mine be the first to fall!"

Cilia raised her Jade Staff, layering an azure gale barrier over their heads.

"Father! If your blade remains blind to the truth, let it strike through my heart first!"

Shoulder to shoulder, the three youths braced against the descending thunderbolt. Fire, light, and wind fused into a blinding supernova across the altar. The devastating stroke broke upon their unified barrier, dissolving into harmless sparks. In the quiet that followed, only their ragged breathing stirred the heights.

The great storm blade slipped from Aeolus's fingers, clattering upon the stones. The gray film clouding his eyes washed away in tears. Stumbling forward, the king cradled Cilia's face in trembling hands.

"Cilia... My precious daughter... I was blinded by sorrow, dancing like a fool to a traitor's whispers... In the eyes of these youths shielding one another, the dream of peace once shared by Sylphid and Aleksandar lives still..."

Kneeling, Aeolus grasped Kadan's calloused hands.

"Young knight, your shield has opened my blind eyes. Sir Aleksandar's noble spirit smiles proudly within you."

Wiping her tears, Cilia supported her father.

"Father, we must halt the spire's collapse! Zephyr is channeling the Nine Shadows Codex!"

Aeolus pointed toward the inner bronze doors with grim urgency.

"Make haste! Zephyr has inverted the mana flows to awaken the abyssal depths! Before the black tsunami drowns the island, race to the core!"

Kadan adjusted his shield with solemn resolve.

"Though the tides rise to the heavens, this shield will never yield."

Blessed by the sovereign of the winds, the three companions sprinted boldly into the core of the raging spire.

King Aeolus draped his mantle of royal azure feathers around Cilia's shoulders. "This mantle was the cherished legacy bequeathed by your mother Sylphid. Step forward now as the true sovereign of the winds, and lead our people into the dawn." Cilia fastened the golden brooch, bowing deeply before her father.
