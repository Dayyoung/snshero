Episode 38: Three Silk Seals

Hercul(049.png) arrived at the Wildland military camp. He disguised himself as an envoy congratulating Fenrir(081.png)'s victory, but in reality he came to deliver the three seals left behind by Kadan(041.png)'s teachers.

Grimli(074.png) and Gawain(045.png) each left one seal before their deaths, and the last one was Hercul(049.png)'s own. The first seal contained a method to neutralize the southern invasion route of the Ironforge(080.png) strategic core. Instead of bursting a river or collapsing a castle, the plan was to shift the date of the supply rune by one day and the large army would stop on its own.

Kaze(044.png) was a unique trick. In the corner of the note, there was a joke written by Kaze(044.png) that heroes fall most easily in front of food tables. Kadan(041.png) choked up while trying to laugh.

The voice of the dead teacher came back more clearly in one crooked line than in a grand will. The second seal displayed a list of Fenrir(081.png)'s close associates and the generals who had been bribed by Lucian(061.png). Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png) has been tracking the Black Arrow within the northern camp for a long time.

Fenrir(081.png)'s desire to conquer was his, but the hand that rushed the southern expedition was Lucian(061.png). The list also included the names of generals who were not purchased. Aila the Demon Hunter(070.png) knew that if suspicion was only spread, the army would collapse on its own.

Establishing who you can trust was as important as finding the traitor. The third seal was empty. Hercul(049.png) asked Kadan(041.png) to choose what to write directly.

The reason the teachers did not leave the correct answer was because if they replaced the last choice, they would once again be making the child an instrument of promise. Kadan(041.png) wrote two sentences on the third silk.

Does not participate in the southern expedition. I leave with my mother. He wrote down his name and delivered it directly to Fenrir(081.png).

After reading the silk, Fenrir(081.png) made Elwin(042.png) stay in the military tent. It was under the pretext of protecting guests, but in reality, they were hostages. He told Kadan(041.png) that he would send his mother home if he became the vanguard of the southern expedition.

Titania(064.png) pointed the knife at his father. “You gave me as a treasure, but now you use my mother as a chain? ” Fenrir(081.png) imprisoned his daughter.

Gilgamesh(047.png) was stripped of his military authority while trying to free his sister. The Conqueror, who was trying to unite his family, was making enemies of those closest to him. Celia(062.png) proposed a plan to Kadan(041.png) to secretly steal Elwin(042.png).

Kadan(041.png) hesitated whether the expeditionary force should set off while rescuing his mother. Elwin(042.png) heard the conversation between the two and called his son. “Your father gave up his life to send me.

I am grateful for that choice, but I have also been trapped in that day for the rest of my life. If you abandon so many people just because you saved me, I end up paying for someone else’s life. ” Elwin(042.png) added his own emblem to the back of the third seal.

I do not buy my son’s judgment with my life. That night, Hercul(049.png), Celia(062.png), Titania(064.png), and Gilgamesh(047.png) shared roles.

It was decided to change the supply rune, notify the southern tribes of the invasion, and open Elwin(042.png)'s detention barracks. Kadan(041.png) attended Fenrir(081.png)'s meeting and wasted time. The plan was almost successful, but the buyer of Lucian(061.png) discovered Elwin(042.png).

He put a knife to his mother's throat to force Kadan(041.png) into submission. Elwin(042.png) trembled with fear but held onto the blade himself. “Kadan(041.png), don’t make your decision based on me.

Look at the people you said you would protect. ” She pulled the soldier's sword to her chest. When Kadan(041.png) came running, Elwin(042.png) was already in Celia(062.png)'s arms.

Finally, he stroked his son's face and thanked him for being the one who chose truth over revenge. Kadan(041.png) tried to freeze the wound, but Elwin(042.png) blocked his hand. He told me not to use my remaining strength on myself, but to freeze the alarm tower to give myself time to escape.

Kadan(041.png) did not want to accept the last request as a tactic, but Celia(062.png) turned his hand towards the alarm tower. While ice covered the tower, Elwin(042.png) thanked Titania(064.png) for taking care of his son in the north, and asked Celia(062.png) to always tell Kadan(041.png) when he was wrong in the future. It was not a way to choose a woman to stay by his son's side, but a farewell that respected both people's choices.

The military camp alarm went off. Kadan(041.png) didn't even get time to hug her mother and cry. He had to escape to the south with the three seals in his arms.
