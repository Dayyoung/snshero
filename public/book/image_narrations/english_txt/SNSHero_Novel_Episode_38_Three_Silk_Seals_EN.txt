SNSHero Novel
Episode 38: Three Silk Seals

The grand throne room atop the Citadel of Ruin was engulfed in a maelstrom of corrupted crimson mana. Upon the obsidian dais sat High Chancellor Lucian, who had cast aside every vestige of humanity. From his arched spine sprouted the venomous serpentine wings of Medusa Gorgo and the curled black horns of Belphegor, while hovering before his gauntlets were the Three Silk Seals of the Nine Shadows Codex, burning with oily black smoke.

Lucian rose from his seat, his forked tongue tasting the brimstone air.

"Hahaha! You have crawled like vermin before my throne at last! Yet you are too late! When these three seals dissolve, the entire realm shall sink into the bottomless abyss!"

With a savage hiss, Lucian tore the first silk seal. From the obsidian floor erupted dozens of barbed crimson tentacles, weeping corrosive bile that hissed upon the flagstones.

Kadan braced his footing, bringing the Dragon Breaker shield forward.

"Your monstrous ambition ends here! So long as this shield stands, the light of this continent shall never flicker out!"

Cilia pointed her staff toward the ceiling vaults.

"With every seal that unbinds, an abyssal monarch descends! We cannot afford a heartbeat of hesitation!"

The barbed tentacles descended in a vicious volley, striking like steel ballista bolts. Yet Kadan channeled star-breathing through his vanguard, the adamantite plate of his shield scattering the acid in blinding golden sparks.

"The illusions of your darkness are broken! Behold the light of truth!"

Leaping into the air, Cilia swung her jade staff in a wide arc.

"Blades of the southern gale, sever the first chain of curse!"

A crescent of concentrated wind cleaved through the gloom, striking the first silk seal dead center. With a shriek like tearing iron, the seal burst into fragments, and the flailing tentacles turned to grey ash.

Lucian snarled, tearing the second silk seal in a frenzy. From the ceiling plunged the phantom manifestation of Medusa Gorgo—a monstrous serpent with dozens of glowing crimson eyes, spewing beams of petrifying light. Marble pillars struck by the rays turned to chalky limestone, crumbling into dust.

Kadan threw himself in front of Cilia, planting his shield into the stone.

"Not a hair upon her head shall be touched while I draw breath!"

The petrifying beam struck the Dragon Breaker shield dead center. As the adamantite plate began to turn grey under the ancient curse, Kadan unsheathed the Silver Dagger and drove its tip into the shield's core. Master Aleksandar's paladin runes roared to life, reversing the petrification in a blast of sacred white flame that shattered the serpent's skull and obliterated the second seal.

Cilia called out in triumph.

"The second seal has fallen! Only the dark core within Lucian's heart remains!"

Black blood erupted from Lucian's chest as the backdraft of corrupted mana tore his flesh. Driven to utter madness, the tyrant plunged the third silk seal directly into his own heart.

"If I must perish, you shall be dragged into the underworld with me! Lord Belphegor, accept my mortal soul and pull this world into eternal damnation!"

A dimensional singularity tore open within Lucian's chest, threatening to swallow the citadel and the capital into the void.

Cilia gripped her staff, her voice urgent.

"The third seal is fused to his life force! Unless we pierce that core, the entire mountain will collapse into the void! Kadan, I trust your shield and blade!"

Kadan set his jaw, pressing Ignis's White Glass Ember against the crest of his shield.

"I shall open the vanguard. Support my back with your wind, Cilia!"

Pure white sanctifying flame, golden starlight mana, and Cilia's jade wind converged into a towering spear of incandescent brilliance that enveloped Kadan's silhouette.

"In the name of every life we have defended, pierce the heart of darkness!"

Kadan charged directly into the vortex. The Dragon Breaker shield smashed through the dark barrier, and the Silver Dagger drove through the glowing core of the third silk seal.

A blinding explosion of immaculate light filled the hall. The third seal shattered, Lucian's dark mana dissolved into nothingness, and the cursed wards of the Nine Shadows Codex dispersed forever on the morning gale.
